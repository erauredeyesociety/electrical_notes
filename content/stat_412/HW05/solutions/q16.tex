\section*{Problem}
Freshman aptitude scores have $\mu=540$ and $\sigma=50$. Two groups of sizes
$72$ and $50$ are selected. Find the probability their sample means differ by
(a) more than $15$ points and (b) an amount between $2$ and $6$ points.

\section*{Work}
For $D=\bar X_1-\bar X_2$,
\[
\mu_D=0,\qquad
\sigma_D=\sqrt{\frac{50^2}{72}+\frac{50^2}{50}}\approx9.2045.
\]
For part (a),
\[
z=\frac{15}{9.2045}\approx1.63,
\]
so
\[
P(|D|>15)=2P(Z>1.63)=2(1-0.9484)=0.1032.
\]
For part (b),
\[
z_1=\frac{2}{9.2045}\approx0.22,\qquad
z_2=\frac{6}{9.2045}\approx0.65.
\]
Thus
\[
P(2<|D|<6)=2[\Phi(0.65)-\Phi(0.22)]
=2(0.7422-0.5871)=0.3102.
\]

\finalanswer{(a) $0.1032$; (b) $0.3102$.}
