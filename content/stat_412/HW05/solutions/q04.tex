\section*{Problem}
The tar contents, in milligrams, are
\[
11.6,11.4,8.6,13.8,12.4,14.6,12.3,9.7.
\]
Find the mean and variance.

\section*{Work}
\[
\bar x=\frac{11.6+11.4+8.6+13.8+12.4+14.6+12.3+9.7}{8}
=11.80.
\]
Because the brands were selected at random, use the sample variance:
\[
s^2=\frac{\sum (x_i-\bar x)^2}{n-1}
=\frac{27.3}{7}=3.90.
\]

\finalanswer{(a) $\bar x=11.80$ mg; (b) $s^2=3.90$.}
