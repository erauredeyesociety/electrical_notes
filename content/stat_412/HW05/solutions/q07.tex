\section*{Problem}
For samples of size $49$, the standard deviation of $\bar X$ is $4$. How large
must $n$ become to reduce the standard deviation of $\bar X$ to $3.5$?

\section*{Work}
The standard error has form $\sigma_{\bar X}=\sigma/\sqrt n$. First recover the
population standard deviation:
\[
4=\frac{\sigma}{\sqrt{49}}=\frac{\sigma}{7}
\quad\Rightarrow\quad \sigma=28.
\]
Now solve
\[
3.5=\frac{28}{\sqrt n}
\quad\Rightarrow\quad
\sqrt n=8
\quad\Rightarrow\quad
n=64.
\]

\finalanswer{$n=64$.}
