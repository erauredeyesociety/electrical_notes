\section*{Problem}
The GPAs are
\[
2.5,3.5,3.4,2.9,1.7,2.1,2.9,1.8,2.6,2.5,
2.7,2.4,3.2,2.5,1.9,2.4,3.6,2.3,2.5,3.5.
\]
Calculate the sample standard deviation.

\section*{Work}
For sample data,
\[
s=\sqrt{\frac{\sum (x_i-\bar x)^2}{n-1}}.
\]
Here $n=20$, $\bar x=2.665$, and $\sum (x_i-\bar x)^2\approx6.1695$, so
\[
s=\sqrt{\frac{6.1695}{19}}\approx0.5698\approx0.57.
\]

\finalanswer{$s\approx0.57$.}
