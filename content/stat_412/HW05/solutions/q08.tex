\section*{Problem}
A thread has mean tensile strength $80.5$ kg and standard deviation $5.4$ kg.
How is the variance of the sample mean changed when the sample size is
(a) increased from $36$ to $324$ and (b) decreased from $900$ to $81$?

\section*{Work}
\[
\operatorname{Var}(\bar X)=\frac{\sigma^2}{n}
=\frac{5.4^2}{n}=\frac{29.16}{n}.
\]
For part (a),
\[
n=36:\ \frac{29.16}{36}=0.81,\qquad
n=324:\ \frac{29.16}{324}=0.09.
\]
Both are variances, so both units are $\text{kg}^2$.
For part (b),
\[
n=900:\ \frac{29.16}{900}=0.0324,\qquad
n=81:\ \frac{29.16}{81}=0.36.
\]

\finalanswer{(a) The variance decreases from $0.81\text{ kg}^2$ to $0.09\text{ kg}^2$.
(b) The variance increases from $0.0324\text{ kg}^2$ to $0.36\text{ kg}^2$.}
