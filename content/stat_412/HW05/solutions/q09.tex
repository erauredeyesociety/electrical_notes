\section*{Problem}
For the discrete uniform population $x=5,13,21$ with probability $1/3$ each,
find $P(13.3<\bar X<14.5)$ for samples of size $96$, with means measured to the
nearest tenth.

\section*{Work}
The population mean is
\[
\mu=\frac{5+13+21}{3}=13.
\]
The population variance is
\[
\sigma^2=\frac{(5-13)^2+(13-13)^2+(21-13)^2}{3}
=\frac{128}{3}.
\]
Thus
\[
\sigma_{\bar X}=\sqrt{\frac{\sigma^2}{n}}
=\sqrt{\frac{128/3}{96}}=\frac{2}{3}.
\]
Because means are measured to the nearest tenth, use boundaries $13.35$ and
$14.45$:
\[
z_1=\frac{13.35-13}{2/3}\approx0.53,\qquad
z_2=\frac{14.45-13}{2/3}\approx2.18.
\]
Using the z-table,
\[
P= \Phi(2.18)-\Phi(0.53)=0.9854-0.7019=0.2835.
\]

\finalanswer{$0.2835$.}
