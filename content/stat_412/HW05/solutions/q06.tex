\section*{Problem}
Samples of size $64$ are drawn from a normal population with $\mu=50$ and
$\sigma=12$. Find the probability that $\bar X$ falls from
$\mu_{\bar X}-1.3\sigma_{\bar X}$ to $\mu_{\bar X}-0.2\sigma_{\bar X}$.

\section*{Work}
Since the interval is already written in standard-error units,
\[
P(\mu_{\bar X}-1.3\sigma_{\bar X}<\bar X<\mu_{\bar X}-0.2\sigma_{\bar X})
=P(-1.3<Z<-0.2).
\]
Using the standard normal table,
\[
P(-1.3<Z<-0.2)=\Phi(-0.2)-\Phi(-1.3)=0.4207-0.0968=0.3239.
\]

\finalanswer{$0.3239$.}
