\section*{Problem}
Alloy A and alloy B each have known standard deviation $5$ tons, with samples of
size $50$ from each. Part (a) asks for
$P(\bar X_A-\bar X_B>3\mid \mu_A=\mu_B)$.
Part (b) asks whether these data strongly support alloy A.

\section*{Work}
Let $D=\bar X_A-\bar X_B$. If $\mu_A=\mu_B$, then
\[
\mu_D=0,
\qquad
\sigma_D=\sqrt{\frac{5^2}{50}+\frac{5^2}{50}}
=\sqrt{1}=1.
\]
Thus
\[
z=\frac{3-0}{1}=3.
\]
From the z-table,
\[
P(D>3)=P(Z>3)=1-0.9987=0.0013.
\]
This probability is negligible, so the observed sample difference would be very
unlikely if the population means were equal.

\finalanswer{(a) $0.0013$.
(b) Select: probability \textbf{is} negligible; the experiment \textbf{supports} alloy A.}
