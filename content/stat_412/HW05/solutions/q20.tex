\section*{Problem}
Let $X$ be normal with mean $11$ and variance $4$. Find the sample size $n$
needed so that
\[
P(10.9\le\bar X\le11.1)=0.975.
\]
Round up to the nearest whole number.

\section*{Work}
Since $\sigma^2=4$, $\sigma=2$, and
\[
\sigma_{\bar X}=\frac{2}{\sqrt n}.
\]
The interval is symmetric about $11$ with half-width $0.1$, so
\[
P\left(|\bar X-11|\le0.1\right)=0.975.
\]
The corresponding standard-normal cutoff satisfies
\[
\Phi(z)=\frac{1+0.975}{2}=0.9875,
\]
which is $z=2.24$ on the linked z-table. Now solve
\[
z=\frac{0.1}{2/\sqrt n}=0.05\sqrt n.
\]
Thus
\[
\sqrt n=\frac{2.24}{0.05}=44.8,
\qquad
n=2007.04.
\]
Rounding up gives $n=2008$.

\finalanswer{$n=2008$.}
