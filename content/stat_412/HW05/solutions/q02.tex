\section*{Problem}
For the phosphate data, find the mean, median, and mode:
\[
53,50,47,47,44,36,31,29,26,26,26,22.
\]

\section*{Work}
\[
\bar x=\frac{437}{12}=36.4167\approx36.42.
\]
Sorted data:
\[
22,26,26,26,29,31,36,44,47,47,50,53.
\]
With $n=12$, the median is the average of the 6th and 7th values:
\[
\frac{31+36}{2}=33.5.
\]
The most frequent value is $26$.

\finalanswer{(a) Mean $=36.42$ g; (b) median $=33.5$ g; (c) mode $=26$ g.}
