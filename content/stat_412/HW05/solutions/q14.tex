\section*{Problem}
Sample 1 has $n_1=36$, $\mu_1=80$, $\sigma_1=3$. Sample 2 has $n_2=81$,
$\mu_2=65$, $\sigma_2=5$. Find the probability that
$\bar X_1-\bar X_2$ is at least $13.7$ but less than $15.6$, measured to the
nearest tenth.

\section*{Work}
\[
\mu_{\bar X_1-\bar X_2}=80-65=15,
\]
and
\[
\sigma_{\bar X_1-\bar X_2}
=\sqrt{\frac{3^2}{36}+\frac{5^2}{81}}
\approx0.7474.
\]
Because differences are measured to the nearest tenth, use $13.65$ and $15.55$:
\[
z_1=\frac{13.65-15}{0.7474}\approx-1.81,\qquad
z_2=\frac{15.55-15}{0.7474}\approx0.74.
\]
From the z-table,
\[
P=0.7704-0.0351=0.7353.
\]

\finalanswer{$0.7353$.}
