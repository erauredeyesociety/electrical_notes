\section*{Problem}
Electrical resistors have mean $41$ ohms and standard deviation $3$ ohms. Find
the probability that the combined resistance of a random sample of $36$
resistors is more than $1494$ ohms.

\section*{Work}
Let $S$ be the sum of the 36 resistances. Then
\[
\mu_S=36(41)=1476,\qquad \sigma_S=\sqrt{36}(3)=18.
\]
Thus
\[
z=\frac{1494-1476}{18}=1.00.
\]
Using the z-table,
\[
P(S>1494)=P(Z>1.00)=1-0.8413=0.1587.
\]

\finalanswer{$0.1587$.}
