\section*{Problem}
The waiting times are
\[
5,7,7,8,13,7,16,15,15,15.
\]
Treating the data as a random sample, find the range and standard deviation.

\section*{Work}
The range is
\[
R=\max-\min=16-5=11.
\]
For the sample standard deviation,
\[
s=\sqrt{\frac{\sum (x_i-\bar x)^2}{n-1}}.
\]
Here $\bar x=10.8$ and $\sum (x_i-\bar x)^2=169.6$, so
\[
s=\sqrt{\frac{169.6}{9}}\approx4.34.
\]

\finalanswer{(a) $R=11$; (b) $s\approx4.34$.}
