certain brand and records the tread wear in kilometers, as shown below.

`61,000, 52,000, 69,000, 44,000, 59,000, 54,000, 62,000, 68,000, 53,000, 67,000`

500 and then subtracting 125.

subtracting 125:

- Round to three decimal places as needed.
