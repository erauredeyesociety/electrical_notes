# Question 7

The lengths of time, in minutes, that 10 patients waited in a doctor's office
before receiving treatment were recorded as shown below.

`7, 11, 10, 3, 19, 11, 3, 11, 3, 6`

Treating the data as a random sample, find:

(a) the mean;

(b) the median;

(c) the mode.

Round the mean and median to two decimal places as needed. Do not round the
mode.
