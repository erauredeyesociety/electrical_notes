# Question 3

The distribution of heights of a certain breed of terrier has a mean of 71
centimeters and a standard deviation of 11 centimeters, whereas the distribution
of heights of a certain breed of poodle has a mean of 27 centimeters with a
standard deviation of 5 centimeters.

Assuming that the sample means can be measured to any degree of accuracy, find
the probability that the sample mean for a random sample of heights of 64
terriers exceeds the sample mean for a random sample of heights of 100 poodles
by at most 44.2 centimeters.

Round to four decimal places as needed.
