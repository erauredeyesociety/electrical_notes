# Question 2

The amount of time that a drive-through bank teller spends on a customer is a
random variable with mean mu = 9.6 minutes and standard deviation sigma = 4.0
minutes. If a random sample of 64 customers is observed, find the probability
that their mean time at the teller's window is:

(a) at most 8.5 minutes;

(b) more than 10.4 minutes;

(c) at least 9.6 minutes but less than 10.6 minutes.

Round to four decimal places as needed.
