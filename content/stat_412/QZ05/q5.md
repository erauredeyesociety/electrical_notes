# Question 5

The heights of 1000 students are approximately normally distributed with a mean
of 172.1 centimeters and a standard deviation of 6.8 centimeters. Suppose 200
random samples of size 25 are drawn from this population and the means are
recorded to the nearest tenth of a centimeter. Complete parts (a) through (c)
below.

(a) Determine the mean and standard deviation of the sampling distribution of
x-bar.

- Type an integer or a decimal. Do not round.

(b) Determine the expected number of sample means that fall between 169.7 and
173.2 centimeters inclusive.

- Round to the nearest whole number as needed.

(c) Determine the expected number of sample means falling below 169.5
centimeters.

- Round to the nearest whole number as needed.
