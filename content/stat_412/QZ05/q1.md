# Question 1

The mean score for freshmen on an aptitude test at a certain college is 560,
with a standard deviation of 60. Assume the means can be measured to any degree
of accuracy.

What is the probability that two groups selected at random, consisting of 48
and 60 students, respectively, will differ in their mean scores by:

(a) more than 17 points?

(b) an amount between 4 and 9 points?

Round to four decimal places as needed.
