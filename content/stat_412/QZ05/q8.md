# Question 8

The reaction times for a random sample of 9 subjects to a stimulant were
recorded as shown below.

`3.2, 2.7, 2.3, 3.6, 4.9, 4.2, 4.8, 2.6, 4.1`

Calculate:

(a) the range;

(b) the variance using the formula

```text
s^2 = (1/(n-1)) * sum from i=1 to n of (x_i - x-bar)^2
```

For the range, type an integer or a decimal. Do not round. For the variance,
round to two decimal places as needed.
