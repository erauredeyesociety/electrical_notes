\section*{Problem}
The mean score for freshmen on an aptitude test is $560$, with standard
deviation $60$. Two independent groups of sizes $48$ and $60$ are selected.
Find the probability that the two sample means differ by (a) more than $17$
points and (b) an amount between $4$ and $9$ points.

\section*{Work}
Let $D=\bar X_1-\bar X_2$. Since both groups come from the same population,
the expected difference in sample means is $0$:
\[
\mu_D=E(\bar X_1-\bar X_2)=560-560=0.
\]
For independent samples, variances add:
\[
\operatorname{Var}(D)=\operatorname{Var}(\bar X_1)+\operatorname{Var}(\bar X_2)
=\frac{60^2}{48}+\frac{60^2}{60}.
\]
Therefore
\[
\sigma_D=\sqrt{\frac{60^2}{48}+\frac{60^2}{60}}
=\sqrt{75+60}=\sqrt{135}\approx 11.619.
\]
For part (a), ``differ by more than $17$'' means $|D|>17$:
\[
z=\frac{17}{11.619}\approx1.46.
\]
Because the normal distribution is symmetric,
\[
P(|D|>17)=P(D<-17)+P(D>17)=2P(Z>1.46).
\]
Using the standard-normal table, $\Phi(1.46)=0.9279$, so
\[
2P(Z>1.46)=2(1-0.9279)=2(0.0721)=0.1442.
\]
For part (b),
\[
P(4<|D|<9)=2\left[P\left(Z<\frac9{11.619}\right)
-P\left(Z<\frac4{11.619}\right)\right].
\]
The z-scores are
\[
\frac4{11.619}\approx0.34,\qquad \frac9{11.619}\approx0.77.
\]
From the table, $\Phi(0.77)=0.7794$ and $\Phi(0.34)=0.6331$, so
\[
2(0.7794-0.6331)=0.2926.
\]

\finalanswer{(a) $0.1442$ \quad (b) $0.2926$}
