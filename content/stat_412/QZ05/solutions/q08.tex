\section*{Problem}
Reaction times are
\[
3.2,\ 2.7,\ 2.3,\ 3.6,\ 4.9,\ 4.2,\ 4.8,\ 2.6,\ 4.1.
\]
Find the range and the sample variance.

\section*{Work}
The range is
\[
\text{maximum}-\text{minimum}=4.9-2.3=2.6.
\]
The sample mean is
\[
\bar x=\frac{3.2+2.7+2.3+3.6+4.9+4.2+4.8+2.6+4.1}{9}
=\frac{32.4}{9}=3.6.
\]
Now compute the squared deviations from $3.6$:
\[
\begin{array}{c|c|c}
x_i & x_i-\bar x & (x_i-\bar x)^2\\ \hline
3.2 & -0.4 & 0.16\\
2.7 & -0.9 & 0.81\\
2.3 & -1.3 & 1.69\\
3.6 & 0 & 0\\
4.9 & 1.3 & 1.69\\
4.2 & 0.6 & 0.36\\
4.8 & 1.2 & 1.44\\
2.6 & -1.0 & 1.00\\
4.1 & 0.5 & 0.25
\end{array}
\]
The squared deviations sum to
\[
\sum (x_i-3.6)^2=7.40.
\]
Using $s^2=\frac1{n-1}\sum (x_i-\bar x)^2$ with $n=9$,
\[
s^2=\frac{7.40}{8}=0.925\approx0.93.
\]

\finalanswer{Range $=2.6$; sample variance $s^2=0.93$.}
