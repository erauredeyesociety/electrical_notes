\section*{Problem}
Tread-wear data, in kilometers:
\[
61000,\ 52000,\ 69000,\ 44000,\ 59000,\ 54000,\ 62000,\ 68000,\ 53000,\ 67000.
\]
First divide each observation by $500$ and subtract $125$. Find the variance of
the transformed data, then use it to find the standard deviation of the
original data.

\section*{Work}
Let
\[
Y=\frac{X}{500}-125.
\]
The transformed values are
\[
-3,\ -21,\ 13,\ -37,\ -7,\ -17,\ -1,\ 11,\ -19,\ 9.
\]
The sample variance of these transformed values is
\[
s_Y^2=263.511.
\]
The units of $s_Y^2$ are transformed-units squared. They are not kilometers or
square kilometers yet, because the data have been rescaled and shifted.

The transformation is
\[
Y=\frac{1}{500}X-125.
\]
Subtracting $125$ does not change variance. Multiplying by $\frac1{500}$ makes
the variance $\left(\frac1{500}\right)^2$ times as large:
\[
s_Y^2=\left(\frac1{500}\right)^2s_X^2.
\]
Solving for the original variance gives
\[
s_X^2=500^2s_Y^2.
\]
The standard deviation is the square root of the original variance:
\[
s_X=\sqrt{s_X^2}=\sqrt{500^2s_Y^2}
=500\sqrt{s_Y^2}.
\]
Thus the multiple-choice wording ``multiply by \_\_, then find the square root''
should use $500^2=250000$ in the blank:
\[
s_X=\sqrt{263.511(250000)}
=8116.5\approx8117.
\]
Because this is a standard deviation, the final units are kilometers, not
square kilometers. Square kilometers would be the unit for the original
variance $s_X^2$.

\finalanswer{Transformed variance $=263.511$; choose B: multiply by
$500^2=250000$, then find the square root; standard deviation $=8117$ km.}
