\section*{Problem}
The wait times are
\[
7,\ 11,\ 10,\ 3,\ 19,\ 11,\ 3,\ 11,\ 3,\ 6.
\]
Find the mean, median, and mode.

\section*{Work}
There are $n=10$ observations. Add them:
\[
7+11+10+3+19+11+3+11+3+6=84.
\]
\[
\bar x=\frac{7+11+10+3+19+11+3+11+3+6}{10}
=\frac{84}{10}=8.40.
\]
Sorted data:
\[
3,\ 3,\ 3,\ 6,\ 7,\ 10,\ 11,\ 11,\ 11,\ 19.
\]
The median is the average of the 5th and 6th values:
\[
\tilde x=\frac{7+10}{2}=8.50.
\]
For the mode, count repeated values:
\[
3\text{ appears 3 times},\qquad 11\text{ appears 3 times}.
\]
No other value appears as often, so there is more than one mode.

\finalanswer{Mean $=8.40$; median $=8.50$; choose ``more than one mode,'' modes
$3,11$.}
