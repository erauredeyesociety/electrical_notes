\section*{Problem}
Drive-through bank teller time has mean $\mu=9.6$ minutes and standard
deviation $\sigma=4.0$ minutes. For a random sample of $64$ customers, find
the probability that the sample mean is (a) at most $8.5$, (b) more than
$10.4$, and (c) at least $9.6$ but less than $10.6$ minutes.

\section*{Work}
For a sample mean, the mean stays the same and the standard deviation becomes
the standard error:
\[
\mu_{\bar X}=9.6,\qquad \sigma_{\bar X}=\frac{4.0}{\sqrt{64}}=0.5.
\]
(a)
\[
z=\frac{8.5-9.6}{0.5}=\frac{-1.1}{0.5}=-2.20.
\]
Thus
\[
P(\bar X\le8.5)=P(Z\le-2.20)=0.0139.
\]
(b)
\[
z=\frac{10.4-9.6}{0.5}=\frac{0.8}{0.5}=1.60.
\]
From the table, $\Phi(1.60)=0.9452$, so
\[
P(\bar X>10.4)=P(Z>1.60)=1-0.9452=0.0548.
\]
(c)
\[
z_1=\frac{9.6-9.6}{0.5}=0,\qquad
z_2=\frac{10.6-9.6}{0.5}=2.00.
\]
Thus
\[
P(9.6\le\bar X<10.6)=0.9772-0.5000=0.4772.
\]

\finalanswer{(a) $0.0139$ \quad (b) $0.0548$ \quad (c) $0.4772$}
