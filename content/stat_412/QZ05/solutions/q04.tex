\section*{Problem}
A machine makes resistors with mean resistance $40$ ohms and standard deviation
$3$ ohms. Find the probability that a random sample of $36$ resistors has
combined resistance more than $1476$ ohms.

\section*{Work}
Let $S=X_1+\cdots+X_{36}$ be the combined resistance. For a sum of independent
resistors, means add and variances add:
\[
\mu_S=36(40)=1440.
\]
The variance of one resistor is $3^2=9$, so
\[
\operatorname{Var}(S)=36(9)=324,\qquad \sigma_S=\sqrt{324}=18.
\]
Then
\[
z=\frac{1476-1440}{18}=2.00.
\]
So
\[
P(S>1476)=P(Z>2.00)=1-0.9772=0.0228.
\]

\finalanswer{$0.0228$}
