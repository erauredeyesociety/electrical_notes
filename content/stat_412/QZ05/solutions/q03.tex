\section*{Problem}
Terrier heights have mean $71$ cm and standard deviation $11$ cm. Poodle
heights have mean $27$ cm and standard deviation $5$ cm. Find the probability
that the mean of $64$ terriers exceeds the mean of $100$ poodles by at most
$44.2$ cm.

\section*{Work}
Let $D=\bar X_T-\bar X_P$, where $T$ denotes terriers and $P$ denotes poodles.
The mean difference is
\[
\mu_D=71-27=44.
\]
Because the two samples are independent, the variance of the difference is the
sum of the sampling variances:
\[
\operatorname{Var}(D)=\frac{11^2}{64}+\frac{5^2}{100}.
\]
So
\[
\sigma_D=\sqrt{\frac{11^2}{64}+\frac{5^2}{100}}
\approx\sqrt{1.890625+0.25}
\approx1.4631.
\]
We need $P(D\le44.2)$:
\[
z=\frac{44.2-44}{1.4631}=\frac{0.2}{1.4631}\approx0.14.
\]
From the standard-normal table, $\Phi(0.14)=0.5557$.

\finalanswer{$0.5557$}
