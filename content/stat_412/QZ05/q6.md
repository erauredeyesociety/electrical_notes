# Question 6

6500 psi and standard deviation 200 psi. A random sample of 64 rivets is taken.
Consider the distribution of x-bar, the sample mean breaking strength.

(a) What is the probability that the sample mean falls between 6440 psi and 6560
psi?

- Round to four decimal places as needed.

(b) What sample n would be necessary in order to have
P(6470 < x-bar < 6530) = 0.90?

- Round up to the nearest whole number.
