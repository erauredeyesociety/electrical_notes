# Question 11

Let \(X\) represent the number that occurs when an 8-sided red die is tossed,
and let \(Y\) represent the number that occurs when an 8-sided green die is
tossed. Complete parts (a) and (b).

(a) Find the variance of \(4X-Y\).

(b) Find the variance of \(X+3Y-5\).

(Simplify your answers.)

Each die is uniform on \(1,\ldots,8\), so

\[
\operatorname{Var}(X)=\operatorname{Var}(Y)
=\frac{8^2-1}{12}=5.25.
\]

Thus the answers are \(89.25\) and \(52.5\), respectively.
