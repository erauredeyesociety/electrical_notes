# Question 14

A random variable \(X\) has mean \(\mu=4\) and variance \(\sigma^2=4\).
Use Chebyshev's theorem to answer parts (a) through (d).

Since \(\sigma=2\), Chebyshev's theorem gives

\[
P(|X-4|\ge t)\le \frac{4}{t^2}.
\]

## (a)

Find \(P(|X-4|\ge3)\).

\[
\boxed{P(|X-4|\ge3)\le\frac49}.
\]

## (b)

Find \(P(|X-4|<3)\).

\[
\boxed{P(|X-4|<3)\ge1-\frac49=\frac59}.
\]

## (c)

Find \(P(-1<X<9)\). Since this event is \(|X-4|<5\),

\[
\boxed{P(-1<X<9)\ge1-\frac4{25}=\frac{21}{25}}.
\]

Parts (a), (b), and (c) were platform-confirmed.

## (d)

Find \(c\) such that

\[
P(|X-4|\ge c)\le0.04.
\]

Set the Chebyshev bound equal to \(0.04=1/25\):

\[
\frac4{c^2}=\frac1{25}
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
c^2=100
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
c=10.
\]

**Enter:** \(\boxed{10}\).
