# Question 14

A random variable \(X\) has a mean \(\mu=4\) and a variance \(\sigma^2=4\). Using
Chebyshev's theorem, complete parts (a) through (d).

(a) Find \(P(|X-4|\ge 3)\). → \(P(|X-4|\ge 3)\ \le\ \dfrac{4}{9}\).

(b) Find \(P(|X-4|<3)\). → this is the complement, so Chebyshev gives a
**lower** bound: \(P(|X-4|<3)\ \ge\ 1-\dfrac{4}{9}=\dfrac{5}{9}\).
