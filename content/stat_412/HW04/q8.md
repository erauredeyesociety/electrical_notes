# Question 8

Suppose that \(X\) and \(Y\) are independent random variables having the joint
probability distribution shown below. Complete parts (a) and (b).

| \(f(x,y)\) | \(x=2\) | \(x=4\) |
|---|---|---|
| \(y=1\) | 0.175 | 0.075 |
| \(y=3\) | 0.42 | 0.18 |
| \(y=5\) | 0.105 | 0.045 |

(a) Find \(E(9X-7Y)\). → \(9(2.6)-7(2.8)=3.8\).

(b) Find \(E(XY)\). → since \(X,Y\) are independent, \(E(XY)=E(X)E(Y)
=(2.6)(2.8)=7.28\).

*(Type integers or decimals. Do not round.)*
