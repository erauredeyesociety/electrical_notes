# Question 4

The joint density is

\[
f(x,y)=
\begin{cases}
\dfrac34x, & 0<x<2,\ 0<y<2-x,\\
0, & \text{elsewhere}.
\end{cases}
\]

## (a) Independence

\[
h(y)=\int_0^{2-y}\frac34x\,dx=\frac38(2-y)^2,
\]

so

\[
f(x\mid y)=\frac{f(x,y)}{h(y)}
=\frac{2x}{(2-y)^2},\qquad 0<x<2-y.
\]

This depends on \(y\), so \(X\) and \(Y\) are not independent.

**Select Choice A**, then enter

\[
\boxed{\frac{2x}{(2-y)^2}}.
\]

## (b) Conditional probability

\[
\begin{aligned}
P(X>0.4\mid Y=0.6)
&=\int_{0.4}^{1.4}\frac{2x}{(1.4)^2}\,dx\\
&=\frac{1.4^2-0.4^2}{1.4^2}
=\frac{45}{49}
\approx0.918367.
\end{aligned}
\]

**Enter:** \(\boxed{\frac{45}{49}}\). If the field requires a decimal, enter
\(\boxed{0.918367}\) or the requested rounding.
