# Question 4

The joint density function of the random variables \(X\) and \(Y\) is given to
the right.

\[
f(x,y)=
\begin{cases}
\dfrac34\,x, & 0<x<2,\ 0<y<2-x,\\
0, & \text{elsewhere}.
\end{cases}
\]

(a) Show that \(X\) and \(Y\) are not independent. *(Select the correct choice
and fill in the answer box.)*

(b) Find \(P(X>0.4 \mid Y=0.6)\).

## Answers

- (a) \(X\) and \(Y\) are **not** independent: the conditional
  \(f(x\mid y)=\dfrac{2x}{(2-y)^2}\) (for \(0<x<2-y\)) depends on \(y\), so it is
  not equal to the marginal \(g(x)\).
- (b) \(P(X>0.4\mid Y=0.6)=\dfrac{45}{49}\approx0.9184\).
