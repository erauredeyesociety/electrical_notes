\section*{Problem}
$f(x,y)=\dfrac{64y}{5x^3}$ on $x>4,\ 2<y<3$ (and $0$ elsewhere). Compute the
correlation coefficient $\rho_{XY}$.

\section*{Setup}
$\rho_{XY}=\dfrac{\operatorname{Cov}(X,Y)}{\sigma_X\sigma_Y}$. Before integrating,
check independence: if the density \emph{factors} into a function of $x$ times a
function of $y$ \emph{and} the support is a rectangle, then $X$ and $Y$ are
independent, which forces $\operatorname{Cov}(X,Y)=0$ and hence $\rho_{XY}=0$.

\section*{Work}
The support $\{x>4\}\times\{2<y<3\}$ is a rectangle, and the density factors:
\[
f(x,y)=\underbrace{\frac{32}{x^3}}_{g(x),\ x>4}\cdot
\underbrace{\frac{2y}{5}}_{h(y),\ 2<y<3},
\]
since $\dfrac{32}{x^3}\cdot\dfrac{2y}{5}=\dfrac{64y}{5x^3}=f(x,y)$, and each
factor integrates to $1$ on its range. Therefore $X$ and $Y$ are independent, so
\[
\operatorname{Cov}(X,Y)=0\quad\Longrightarrow\quad \rho_{XY}=0 .
\]

\finalanswer{$\rho_{XY}=0$ ($X$ and $Y$ are independent).}
