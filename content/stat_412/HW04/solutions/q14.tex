\section*{Problem}
A random variable $X$ has mean $\mu=4$ and variance $\sigma^2=4$. Using
Chebyshev's theorem, find $P(|X-4|\ge 3)$.

\section*{Setup}
Chebyshev: $P(|X-\mu|\ge k\sigma)\le \dfrac{1}{k^2}$. Match the deviation to a
multiple of $\sigma=\sqrt{4}=2$: write $3=k\sigma$.

\section*{Work}
\[
k=\frac{3}{\sigma}=\frac{3}{2},\qquad
\textbf{(a)}\quad P(|X-4|\ge 3)\le \frac{1}{k^2}=\frac{1}{(3/2)^2}=\frac{4}{9} .
\]
\textbf{(b)} The event $|X-4|<3$ is the complement, so its Chebyshev bound is a
\emph{lower} bound:
\[
P(|X-4|<3)=1-P(|X-4|\ge 3)\ \ge\ 1-\frac{4}{9}=\frac{5}{9} .
\]

\finalanswer{(a) $P(|X-4|\ge 3)\le \dfrac49$.\quad
(b) $P(|X-4|<3)\ge \dfrac59$.
