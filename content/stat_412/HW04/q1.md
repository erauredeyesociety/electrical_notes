# Question 1

Suppose

\[
f(x,y)=\frac{x+y}{126},
\qquad x=4,5,6,7,\quad y=4,5,6.
\]

## (a)

\[
P(X\le6,Y=6)=\frac{11}{42}.
\]

**Enter:** \(\boxed{\frac{11}{42}}\). Platform-confirmed.

## (b)

\[
P(X>6,Y\le5)=f(7,4)+f(7,5)=\frac{23}{126}.
\]

**Enter:** \(\boxed{\frac{23}{126}}\). Platform-confirmed.

## (c)

\[
\begin{aligned}
P(X>Y)
&=f(5,4)+f(6,4)+f(6,5)+f(7,4)+f(7,5)+f(7,6)\\
&=\frac{9+10+11+11+12+13}{126}
=\frac{11}{21}.
\end{aligned}
\]

**Enter:** \(\boxed{\frac{11}{21}}\). Platform-confirmed.

## (d)

Find \(P(X+Y=13)\). Within the support, the only possible pair is
\((X,Y)=(7,6)\). Therefore

\[
P(X+Y=13)=f(7,6)=\frac{7+6}{126}=\frac{13}{126}.
\]

**Enter:** \(\boxed{\frac{13}{126}}\).
