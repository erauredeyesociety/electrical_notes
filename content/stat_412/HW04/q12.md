# Question 12

A service facility operates with two service lines. The random variables \(X\)
and \(Y\) are the proportions of the time that line 1 and line 2 are in use,
respectively. The joint probability density function for \((X,Y)\) is given
below. Complete parts (a) through (d).

\[
f(x,y)=
\begin{cases}
x^2+2y^2, & 0\le x,y\le 1,\\
0, & \text{elsewhere}.
\end{cases}
\]

(a) Determine whether or not \(X\) and \(Y\) are independent.

**Answer (confirmed by the platform dropdowns):** \(X\) and \(Y\) **are not**
independent, since \(f(x,y)\) **is not** equal to **\(g(x)\,h(y)\)**, where
\(g(x)\) and \(h(y)\) are the **marginal densities** of \(X\) and \(Y\),
respectively. (Here \(g(x)=x^2+\tfrac23\) and \(h(y)=\tfrac13+2y^2\).)
