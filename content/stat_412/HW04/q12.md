# Question 12

A service facility has two lines. Let \(X\) and \(Y\) be the proportions of
time that lines 1 and 2 are in use. Their joint density is

\[
f(x,y)=x^2+2y^2,\qquad 0\le x,y\le1.
\]

The marginal densities are

\[
g(x)=x^2+\frac23,\qquad h(y)=\frac13+2y^2.
\]

## (a) Independence

Select, in order:

\[
\boxed{\text{are not / is not / }g(x)h(y)\text{ / marginal distributions}}.
\]

All four selections were platform-confirmed.

## (b) Expectations

\[
E(X)=\frac7{12},\qquad E(Y)=\frac23,
\]

\[
\boxed{E(X+Y)=\frac54},\qquad
\boxed{E(XY)=\frac38}.
\]

Both answers were platform-confirmed.

## (c) Variances and covariance

\[
E(X^2)=\frac{19}{45},\qquad
\operatorname{Var}(X)
=\frac{19}{45}-\left(\frac7{12}\right)^2
=\boxed{\frac{59}{720}}.
\]

\[
E(Y^2)=\frac{23}{45},\qquad
\operatorname{Var}(Y)
=\frac{23}{45}-\left(\frac23\right)^2
=\boxed{\frac1{15}}.
\]

\[
\operatorname{Cov}(X,Y)
=\frac38-\left(\frac7{12}\right)\left(\frac23\right)
=\boxed{-\frac1{72}}.
\]

All three answers were platform-confirmed.

## (d) Variance of the sum

Find \(\operatorname{Var}(X+Y)\). Because \(X\) and \(Y\) are not independent,
the covariance term must be included:

\[
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{Var}(X+Y)
&=\operatorname{Var}(X)+\operatorname{Var}(Y)
  +2\operatorname{Cov}(X,Y)\\
&=\frac{59}{720}+\frac1{15}
  +2\left(-\frac1{72}\right)\\
&=\frac{59+48-20}{720}
=\frac{87}{720}
=\frac{29}{240}.
\end{aligned}
\]

**Enter:** \(\boxed{\frac{29}{240}}\).

The attempted \(-\frac1{72}\) is only \(\operatorname{Cov}(X,Y)\), not
\(\operatorname{Var}(X+Y)\).
