\textbf{Problem.} Two tire-quality experts examine stacks of tires and assign a
quality rating to each tire on a 3-point scale. Let \(X\) denote the rating
given by expert A and \(Y\) denote the rating given by expert B. The following
table gives the joint distribution for \(X\) and \(Y\). Find \(\mu_X\) and
\(\mu_Y\).

Click the icon to view the joint distribution for \(X\) and \(Y\).
\[
\begin{array}{c|ccc}
f(x,y)&y=1&y=2&y=3\\\hline
x=1&0.21&0.06&0.03\\
x=2&0.08&0.38&0.06\\
x=3&0.02&0.08&0.08
\end{array}.
\]
\(\mu_X=\boxed{\phantom{0.00}}\)

(Type an integer or a decimal. Round to two decimal places as needed.)

\textbf{Solution.} The row sums are \(0.30,0.52,0.18\), and the column sums are
\(0.31,0.52,0.17\). Thus
\[
\mu_X=1(0.30)+2(0.52)+3(0.18)=1.88,
\]
\[
\mu_Y=1(0.31)+2(0.52)+3(0.17)=1.86.
\]
\[
\boxed{\mu_X=1.88,\qquad \mu_Y=1.86}
\]
