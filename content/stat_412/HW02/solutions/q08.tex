\textbf{Problem.} Suppose that for a very large shipment of integrated-circuit
chips, the probability of failure for any one chip is 0.10. Assuming that the
assumptions underlying the binomial distributions are met, find the
probability that at most 2 chips fail in a random sample of 17.

Click here to view page 1 of the table of binomial probability sums.

Click here to view page 2 of the table of binomial probability sums.

The probability at most 2 chips fail is \(\boxed{\phantom{0.0000}}\).

(Round to four decimal places as needed.)

\textbf{Solution.} Let \(X\sim\operatorname{Bin}(17,0.10)\). Then
\[
P(X\le2)=\sum_{x=0}^{2}\binom{17}{x}(0.10)^x(0.90)^{17-x}
=0.7617971888.
\]
Rounded to four decimal places,
\[
\boxed{P(X\le2)=0.7618}.
\]
