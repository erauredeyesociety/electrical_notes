\textbf{Problem.} A certain region is, on average, hit by 6 hurricanes a year.
Find the mean and variance of the random variable \(X\) representing the number
of hurricanes per year to hit the region.

The mean is \(\boxed{\phantom{0}}\).

(Type an integer or a decimal. Do not round.)

\textbf{Solution.} A Poisson random variable with rate \(\lambda\) has
\(E(X)=\lambda\) and \(\operatorname{Var}(X)=\lambda\). Here \(\lambda=6\), so
\[
\boxed{\mu=6,\qquad \sigma^2=6}.
\]
