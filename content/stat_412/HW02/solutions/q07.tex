\textbf{Problem.} A national study that examined attitudes about
antidepressants revealed that approximately 72\% of respondents believe
``antidepressants do not really cure anything, they just cover up the real
trouble.'' If \(X\) represents the number of people who believe that
antidepressants do not cure but only cover up the real problem, find the mean
and variance of \(X\) when 12 people are selected at random.

The mean is \(\boxed{\phantom{0.00}}\).

(Type an integer or a decimal. Do not round.)

\textbf{Solution.} Since \(X\sim\operatorname{Bin}(12,0.72)\),
\[
\mu=np=12(0.72)=8.64,
\]
\[
\sigma^2=np(1-p)=12(0.72)(0.28)=2.4192.
\]
\[
\boxed{\mu=8.64,\qquad \sigma^2=2.4192}
\]
