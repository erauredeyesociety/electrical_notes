\textbf{Problem.} A certain region of a country is, on average, hit by 8
hurricanes a year.

(a) What is the probability that the region will be hit by fewer than 7
hurricanes in a given year?

(b) What is the probability that the region will be hit by anywhere from 4 to
6 hurricanes in a given year?

Click here to view the table of Poisson probability sums.

(a) The probability that the region will be hit by fewer than 7 hurricanes in
a given year is \(\boxed{\phantom{0.0000}}\).

(Round to four decimal places as needed.)

\textbf{Solution.} Let \(X\sim\operatorname{Poisson}(8)\).
\[
P(X<7)=P(X\le6)=\sum_{x=0}^{6}e^{-8}\frac{8^x}{x!}
=0.3133742775\approx0.3134.
\]
\[
P(4\le X\le6)=\sum_{x=4}^{6}e^{-8}\frac{8^x}{x!}
=0.2709941655\approx0.2710.
\]
\[
\boxed{\text{(a) }0.3134\qquad\text{(b) }0.2710}
\]
