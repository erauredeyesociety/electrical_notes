\textbf{Problem.} An inventory study determines that, on average, demands for a
particular item at a warehouse are made 8 times per day.

(a) What is the probability that on a given day this item is requested more
than 4 times?

(b) What is the probability that on a given day this item is not requested?

Click here to view page 2 of the Poisson probability sums table.

(a) The probability that on a given day this item is requested more than 4
times is \(\boxed{\phantom{0.0000}}\).

(Round to four decimal places as needed.)

\textbf{Solution.} Model \(X\) as Poisson with \(\lambda=8\).
\[
P(X>4)=1-P(X\le4)
=1-\sum_{x=0}^{4}e^{-8}\frac{8^x}{x!}
=0.9003675995\approx0.9004.
\]
\[
P(X=0)=e^{-8}=0.0003354626\approx0.0003.
\]
\[
\boxed{\text{(a) }0.9004\qquad\text{(b) }0.0003}
\]
