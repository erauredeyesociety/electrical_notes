\textbf{Problem.} The probability distribution of the discrete random variable
\(X\) is given below.
\[
f(x)=\binom{3}{x}\left(\frac18\right)^x
\left(\frac78\right)^{3-x},\qquad x=0,1,2,3.
\]
Find the mean of \(X\).

The mean of \(X\) is \(\boxed{\phantom{0.000}}\).

(Type an integer or decimal rounded to three decimal places as needed.)

\textbf{Solution.} This is binomial with \(n=3\) and \(p=1/8\). Therefore
\[
E(X)=np=3\left(\frac18\right)=\frac38=0.375.
\]
\[
\boxed{\mu_X=0.375}
\]
