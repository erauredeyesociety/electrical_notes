\textbf{Problem.} Find the probability that a person flipping a coin gets (a)
the eighth head on the tenth flip, and (b) the first head on the third flip.

(a) The probability that a person flipping a coin gets the eighth head on the
tenth flip is \(\boxed{\phantom{0.0000}}\).

(Round to four decimal places as needed.)

\textbf{Solution.}

(a) There must be exactly seven heads in the first nine flips, followed by a head:
\[
P=\binom97\left(\frac12\right)^7\left(\frac12\right)^2
\left(\frac12\right)=\binom97\left(\frac12\right)^{10}
=0.03515625\approx0.0352.
\]

(b) The required sequence is tail, tail, head:
\[
P=\left(\frac12\right)^3=0.125=0.1250.
\]
\[
\boxed{\text{(a) }0.0352\qquad\text{(b) }0.1250}
\]
