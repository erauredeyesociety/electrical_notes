\textbf{Problem.} The probability distribution of \(X\), the number of
imperfections per 10 meters of a synthetic fabric in continuous rolls of
uniform width, is given by the accompanying table. Find the average number of
imperfections per 10 meters of this fabric.
\[
\begin{array}{c|ccccc}x&0&1&2&3&4\\\hline
f(x)&0.44&0.30&0.18&0.07&0.01\end{array}
\]
The average number of imperfections per 10 meters of this fabric is
\(\boxed{\phantom{0.00}}\).

(Type an integer or a decimal. Round to two decimal places as needed.)

\textbf{Solution.}
\[
E(X)=\sum_x xP(X=x)
=0(0.44)+1(0.30)+2(0.18)+3(0.07)+4(0.01)=0.91.
\]
\[
\boxed{\text{Average number of imperfections}=0.91}
\]
