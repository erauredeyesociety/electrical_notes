# Question 11

Use the given confidence interval to find the margin of error and the sample proportion.

Confidence interval:

`(0.761, 0.787)`

The margin of error `E` is requested first.

## Final answers

\[
E=\frac{0.787-0.761}{2}=0.013.
\]

\[
\hat{p}=\frac{0.761+0.787}{2}=0.774.
\]

- Margin of error: **0.013**
- Sample proportion: **0.774**
