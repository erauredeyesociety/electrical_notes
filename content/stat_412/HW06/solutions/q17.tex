\section*{Problem}
For $c=0.95$, $s^2=5.76$, and $n=26$, construct confidence intervals for $\sigma^2$ and $\sigma$.
\section*{Work}
Use $df=25$. For a normal population,
\[
\frac{(n-1)s^2}{\chi^2_R}<\sigma^2<\frac{(n-1)s^2}{\chi^2_L}.
\]
Using 95\% chi-square critical values with $df=25$ gives
\[
3.54<\sigma^2<10.98.
\]
Take square roots for the standard deviation interval:
\[
\sqrt{3.54}<\sigma<\sqrt{10.98},
\]
so
\[
1.88<\sigma<3.31.
\]
\finalanswer{Variance CI $(3.54,10.98)$; SD CI $(1.88,3.31)$.}
