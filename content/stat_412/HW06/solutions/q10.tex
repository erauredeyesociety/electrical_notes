\section*{Problem}
For $n=43$, $\bar x=28.2$, $s=6.14$, decide whether to use normal, $t$, or neither for a 90\% CI.
\section*{Work}
The sample is random and $n=43\ge30$, so the sampling distribution is approximately normal. The population standard deviation $\sigma$ is unknown; we only have $s=6.14$. Therefore use a $t$ distribution.

If the CI is needed, use $df=42$:
\[
E=t_{0.05,42}\frac{6.14}{\sqrt{43}}\approx1.57,
\]
so
\[
28.2\pm1.57=(26.63,29.77).
\]
With 90\% confidence, the population mean BMI is between the bounds of the confidence interval.
\finalanswer{Choice B: use a $t$-distribution. 90\% CI $(26.63,29.77)$. Interpretation choice A.}
