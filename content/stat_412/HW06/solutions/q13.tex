\section*{Problem}
In a survey of $3183$ adults, $1420$ say they have started paying bills online in the last year. Construct a 99\% confidence interval for the population proportion.
\section*{Work}
\[
\hat p=\frac{1420}{3183}\approx0.4461.
\]
For 99\%, $z_{\alpha/2}\approx2.576$:
\[
E=2.576\sqrt{\frac{0.4461(1-0.4461)}{3183}}\approx0.0227.
\]
Thus
\[
0.4461\pm0.0227=(0.4234,0.4688)\approx(0.423,0.469).
\]
The correct interpretation is that, with 99\% confidence, the population proportion of adults who say they have started paying bills online in the last year is between the endpoints.
\finalanswer{$(0.423,0.469)$; interpretation choice B.}
