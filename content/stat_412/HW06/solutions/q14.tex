\section*{Problem}
In a survey of $688$ adults who started bachelor's degree programs six years ago, $382$ now have a bachelor's degree. Construct a 90\% confidence interval for the population proportion.
\section*{Work}
\[
\hat p=\frac{382}{688}\approx0.5552.
\]
For 90\%, $z_{\alpha/2}=1.645$:
\[
E=1.645\sqrt{\frac{0.5552(1-0.5552)}{688}}\approx0.0312.
\]
So
\[
0.5552\pm0.0312=(0.5241,0.5864)\approx(0.524,0.586).
\]
The correct interpretation is that, with 90\% confidence, the population proportion of adults who started bachelor's degree programs six years ago who now have a bachelor's degree is between the endpoints.
\finalanswer{$(0.524,0.586)$; interpretation choice C.}
