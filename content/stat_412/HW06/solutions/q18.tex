\section*{Problem}
For the 17 bolt diameters, construct 95\% confidence intervals for $\sigma^2$ and $\sigma$.
\section*{Work}
The sample variance from the 17 diameters is
\[
s^2\approx0.07698,\qquad df=16.
\]
Use
\[
\frac{(n-1)s^2}{\chi^2_R}<\sigma^2<\frac{(n-1)s^2}{\chi^2_L}.
\]
Substituting the 95\% chi-square critical values for $df=16$ gives
\[
0.0427<\sigma^2<0.1783,
\]
or rounded,
\[
0.043<\sigma^2<0.178.
\]
Taking square roots:
\[
0.207<\sigma<0.422.
\]
The correct interpretation for part (a), the variance interval, is choice D: with 95\% confidence, the population variance is between 0.043 and 0.178.

For part (b), the standard-deviation interval, choose the statement that uses 95\% confidence and says the population standard deviation is between the two endpoints. That is choice C with values 0.207 and 0.422. A raw HTML dump showed choice D selected, but that option says 5\% confidence, so it is not the correct interpretation.

\finalanswer{Variance CI $(0.043,0.178)$; variance interpretation choice D. SD CI $(0.207,0.422)$; SD interpretation choice C with values 0.207 and 0.422.}
