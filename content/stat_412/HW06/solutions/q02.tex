\section*{Problem}
A confidence interval for the mean seedling height is $(4.5,5.1)$ cm. Find the margin of error and sample mean.
\section*{Work}
The margin of error is half the width:
\[
E=\frac{5.1-4.5}{2}=0.3.
\]
The sample mean is the midpoint:
\[
\bar x=\frac{4.5+5.1}{2}=4.8.
\]
\finalanswer{$E=0.3$ cm; $\bar x=4.8$ cm.}
