\section*{Problem}
For the 20 dissolved-organic-carbon values, find (a) the sample mean, (b) the sample standard deviation, and (c) a 98\% confidence interval for the population mean.
\section*{Work}
Add the 20 observations and divide by 20:
\[
\bar x=\frac{347.13}{20}=17.3565\approx17.36.
\]
For the sample standard deviation,
\[
s=\sqrt{\frac{\sum (x_i-\bar x)^2}{n-1}}\approx8.0102\approx8.01.
\]
For part (c), use a $t$ interval because $\sigma$ is unknown and the population is assumed normal. With $df=19$ and 98\% confidence,
\[
t_{0.01,19}\approx2.539.
\]
The margin of error is
\[
E=2.539\frac{8.0102}{\sqrt{20}}\approx4.55.
\]
Thus
\[
17.3565\pm4.5486=(12.8079,21.9051)\approx(12.81,21.91).
\]
\finalanswer{(a) $\bar x=17.36$ mg/L; (b) $s=8.01$ mg/L; (c) 98\% CI $(12.81,21.91)$.}
