\section*{Problem}
For $n=60$, $\bar x=182.7$ cm, and $s=6.9$ cm, construct a 98\% confidence interval for the mean height and state the possible error.
\section*{Work}
The population standard deviation is not given, so use a $t$ interval with
\[
df=60-1=59.
\]
For 98\%, $t_{\alpha/2,59}\approx2.391$:
\[
E=2.391\frac{6.9}{\sqrt{60}}\approx2.13.
\]
Thus
\[
182.7\pm2.13=(180.57,184.83).
\]
The margin of error is the possible size of the error when estimating $\mu$ by $\bar x$.
\finalanswer{$180.57<\mu<184.83$; fill-ins: $\bar x$ is used as an estimate for $\mu$; with 98\% confidence, the error is at most $2.13$ cm.}
