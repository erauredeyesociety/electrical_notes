\section*{Problem}
Find $\chi^2_L$ and $\chi^2_R$ for $c=0.99$ and $n=28$.
\section*{Work}
The degrees of freedom are
\[
df=n-1=27.
\]
For $c=0.99$, $\alpha=0.01$, so each tail has area $\alpha/2=0.005$. The left and right chi-square critical values are
\[
\chi^2_L=\chi^2_{0.005,27}\approx11.808,
\qquad
\chi^2_R=\chi^2_{0.995,27}\approx49.645.
\]
\finalanswer{$\chi^2_L=11.808$, $\chi^2_R=49.645$}
