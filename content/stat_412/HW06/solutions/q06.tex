\section*{Problem}
For $n=26$, $\bar x=31.7$, $s=7.2$, construct an 80\% $t$ confidence interval for the mean commute time.
\section*{Work}
Use $df=25$ and
\[
\bar x\pm t_{\alpha/2,25}\frac{s}{\sqrt n}.
\]
For 80\%, $t_{\alpha/2,25}\approx1.316$:
\[
E=1.316\frac{7.2}{\sqrt{26}}\approx1.86.
\]
Thus
\[
31.7\pm1.86=(29.84,33.56)\approx(29.8,33.6).
\]
The margin of error is $E\approx1.9$ minutes. The correct interpretation is choice C: with 80\% confidence, it can be said that the population mean commute time is between the bounds of the confidence interval.
\finalanswer{CI $(29.8,33.6)$ minutes; margin of error $\approx1.9$ minutes; interpretation choice C.}
