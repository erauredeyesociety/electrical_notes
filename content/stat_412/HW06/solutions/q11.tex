\section*{Problem}
Given the confidence interval $(0.761,0.787)$, find the margin of error and sample proportion.
\section*{Work}
The margin of error is half the width:
\[
E=\frac{0.787-0.761}{2}=0.013.
\]
The sample proportion is the midpoint:
\[
\hat p=\frac{0.761+0.787}{2}=0.774.
\]
\finalanswer{$E=0.013$; $\hat p=0.774$.}
