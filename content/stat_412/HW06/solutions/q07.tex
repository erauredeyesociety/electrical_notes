\section*{Problem}
For $n=11$, $\bar x=416$, $s=190$, find the margin of error and 90\% $t$ confidence interval for the mean cell-phone retail price.
\section*{Work}
Use $df=10$ and $t_{0.05,10}\approx1.812$:
\[
E=1.812\frac{190}{\sqrt{11}}\approx103.83.
\]
Then
\[
416\pm103.83=(312.17,519.83).
\]
With 90\% confidence, the population mean full retail price of cell phones is between the interval endpoints.

For the interpretation, choose the statement about the population mean, not individual phones and not the percentage of sample means. That is choice C with the fill-in 90.

\finalanswer{$E=103.83$ dollars; unit: dollars; 90\% CI $(312.17,519.83)$. Interpretation choice C with fill-in 90.}
