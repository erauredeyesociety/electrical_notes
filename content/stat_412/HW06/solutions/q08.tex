\section*{Problem}
For $n=4$, $\bar x=75$, $s=14$, construct a 99\% $t$ confidence interval for mean repair cost.
\section*{Work}
Use $df=3$ and $t_{0.005,3}\approx5.841$:
\[
E=5.841\frac{14}{\sqrt4}=40.89.
\]
Thus
\[
75\pm40.89=(34.11,115.89).
\]
The correct interpretation is that, with 99\% confidence, the population mean repair cost is between the bounds of the confidence interval.
\finalanswer{$(34.11,115.89)$; interpretation choice D.}
