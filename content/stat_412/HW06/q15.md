# Question 15

In a survey of 2549 adults in a recent year, 1321 say they have made a New Year's resolution.

Construct 90% and 95% confidence intervals for the population proportion. Interpret the results and compare the widths of the confidence intervals.

The 90% confidence interval for the population proportion is requested first. Round to three decimal places as needed.

The 95% confidence interval is requested next. Round to three decimal places as needed.

## Cleaned / confirmed parts

Given:

- \(x=1321\)
- \(n=2549\)
- \(\hat{p}=1321/2549\approx0.5182\)

Platform-confirmed intervals:

- 90% CI: **\((0.502,0.535)\)**
- 95% CI: **\((0.499,0.538)\)**

Interpretation fill-ins:

With the given confidence, it can be said that the **population proportion** of adults who say they have made a New Year's resolution is **between the endpoints** of the given confidence interval.

Width comparison:

- The 95% confidence interval is wider.

The 95% interval is wider because a higher confidence level requires a larger critical value and therefore a larger margin of error.
