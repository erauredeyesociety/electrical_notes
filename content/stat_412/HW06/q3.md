# Question 3

You are given the sample mean and the population standard deviation. Use this information to construct the 90% and 95% confidence intervals for the population mean. Interpret the results and compare the widths of the confidence intervals.

From a random sample of 59 dates, the mean record high daily temperature in a certain city has a mean of `82.38°F`. Assume the population standard deviation is `13.78°F`.

The 90% confidence interval is requested first.

## Final answers

Known population standard deviation, so use \(z\)-intervals.

For 90%:
\[
82.38\pm1.645\frac{13.78}{\sqrt{59}}
=(79.43,85.33).
\]

For 95%:
\[
82.38\pm1.960\frac{13.78}{\sqrt{59}}
=(78.86,85.90).
\]

- 90% CI: **\((79.43,85.33)\)**
- 95% CI: **\((78.86,85.90)\)**
- Wider interval: **95% confidence interval**
- Interpretation choice: **B**

Choice B says you can be 90% confident that the population mean record high temperature is between the bounds of the 90% interval, and 95% confident for the 95% interval.
