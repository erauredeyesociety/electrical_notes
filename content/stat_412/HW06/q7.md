# Question 7

In a random sample of eleven cell phones, the mean full retail price was `$416.00` and the standard deviation was `$190.00`. Assume the population is normally distributed and use the t-distribution to find the margin of error and construct a 90% confidence interval for the population mean. Interpret the results.

Identify the margin of error. Round to two decimal places as needed.

## Cleaned / confirmed parts

Given:

- \(n=11\)
- \(\bar{x}=416\) dollars
- \(s=190\) dollars
- confidence level \(=90\%\)
- \(df=10\)

The margin of error is
\[
E=t_{\alpha/2,df}\frac{s}{\sqrt{n}}
  =t_{0.05,10}\frac{190}{\sqrt{11}}
  \approx 103.83.
\]

Platform-confirmed:

- Margin of error: **103.83**
- Unit dropdown: **dollars**

The 90% confidence interval is
\[
416\pm 103.83=(312.17,519.83).
\]

Interpretation choices:

- A. With ___% confidence, it can be said that most cell phones in the population have full retail prices that are between the interval's endpoints.
- B. ___% of all random samples of eleven phones from the population of cell phones will have a mean full retail price that is between the interval's endpoints.
- C. With ___% confidence, it can be said that the population mean full retail price of cell phones is between the interval's endpoints.
- D. It can be said that ___% of the population of cell phones have full retail prices that are between the interval's endpoints.

Correct interpretation:

- Choice **C**
- Fill in **90**

Confidence intervals for means describe the **population mean**, not most individual phones and not a percentage of all sample means.
