# Question 16

Find the critical values chi-square left and chi-square right for the given confidence level `c` and sample size `n`.

`c = 0.99`, `n = 28`

The left critical value is requested first. Round to three decimal places as needed.

## Final answers

Here \(df=n-1=27\), \(c=0.99\), and \(\alpha=0.01\).

- \(\chi^2_L=11.808\)
- \(\chi^2_R=49.645\)
