# Question 5

A random sample of the closing stock prices, in dollars, for a company in a recent year is listed below. Assume that sigma is `$2.73`. Construct the 90% and 99% confidence intervals for the population mean. Interpret the results and compare the widths of the confidence intervals.

Data:

`21.53, 22.52, 15.23, 22.79, 21.96, 20.92, 17.19, 15.45, 19.16, 21.56, 16.17, 22.52, 22.88, 19.46, 17.81, 22.68`

The 90% confidence interval is requested first. Round to two decimal places.

The 99% confidence interval is requested next. Round to two decimal places.

Interpret the results. Choose the correct statement:

A. 90% of the mean closing stock prices are in the 90% confidence interval and 99% of the mean closing stock prices are in the 99% confidence interval.

B. There is 90% confidence that the mean closing stock price is in the 90% confidence interval and 99% confidence that the mean closing stock price is in the 99% confidence interval.

C. The 90% confidence interval contains the mean closing stock price 90% of the time and the 99% confidence interval contains the mean closing stock price 99% of the time.

D. The probability that the mean closing stock price is in the 90% confidence interval is about 90% and the probability that the mean closing stock price is in the 99% confidence interval is about 99%.

Compare the widths of the intervals.
