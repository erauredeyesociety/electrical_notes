# Question 2

Use the confidence interval to find the estimated margin of error. Then find the sample mean.

A biologist reports a confidence interval of `(4.5, 5.1)` when estimating the mean height, in centimeters, of a sample of seedlings.

The estimated margin of error is requested first.

## Final answers

For a confidence interval \((4.5,5.1)\):

\[
E=\frac{5.1-4.5}{2}=0.3,
\qquad
\bar{x}=\frac{4.5+5.1}{2}=4.8.
\]

- Margin of error: **0.3**
- Sample mean: **4.8**
