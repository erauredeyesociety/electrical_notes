# Question 18

The diameters, in inches, of 17 randomly selected bolts produced by a machine are listed. Use a 95% level of confidence to construct a confidence interval for (a) the population variance \(\sigma^2\) and (b) the population standard deviation \(\sigma\). Interpret the results.

Data:

`4.471, 4.422, 4.024, 4.322, 4.005, 3.766, 3.811, 3.769, 4.246, 3.934, 4.163, 4.528, 3.938, 3.747, 3.849, 3.834, 4.435`

## Cleaned / confirmed parts

The sample variance is approximately
\[
s^2\approx0.07698,
\]
with
\[
df=n-1=16.
\]

Using the chi-square confidence interval formula,
\[
\frac{(n-1)s^2}{\chi^2_R}<\sigma^2<\frac{(n-1)s^2}{\chi^2_L}.
\]

Platform-confirmed variance interval:

- \(\sigma^2\) CI: **\((0.043,0.178)\)**

Variance interpretation choices:

- A. With 5% confidence, it can be said that the population variance is greater than ___.
- B. With 95% confidence, it can be said that the population variance is less than ___.
- C. With 5% confidence, it can be said that the population variance is between ___ and ___.
- D. With 95% confidence, it can be said that the population variance is between ___ and ___.

Correct variance interpretation:

- Choice **D**
- Fill in **0.043** and **0.178**

Taking square roots of the variance interval gives the standard-deviation interval:
\[
\sqrt{0.043}\approx0.207,\qquad \sqrt{0.178}\approx0.422.
\]

More precisely from the unrounded variance endpoints:

- \(\sigma\) CI: **\((0.207,0.422)\)**

Standard-deviation interpretation choices from the raw HTML:

- A. With 5% confidence, the population standard deviation is less than ___ inches.
- B. With 95% confidence, the population standard deviation is greater than ___ inches.
- C. With 95% confidence, the population standard deviation is between ___ and ___ inches.
- D. With 5% confidence, the population standard deviation is between ___ and ___ inches.

Correct standard-deviation interpretation:

- Choice **C**
- Fill in **0.207** and **0.422**

Note: the raw HTML showed choice D selected, but choice D says 5% confidence and is not the correct interpretation for a 95% confidence interval.
