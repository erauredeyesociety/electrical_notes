# Question 7

The accompanying table describes the distribution of eye colors reported by male and female statistics students. Use a 0.01 significance level to test whether there is sufficient evidence to warrant rejection of the belief that gender and eye color are independent traits.

| Gender | Blue | Brown | Green | Hazel |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Male | 357 | 282 | 117 | 156 |
| Female | 380 | 357 | 202 | 186 |

## Answer

\[
H_0:\text{gender and eye color are independent traits.}
\]

\[
H_1:\text{gender and eye color are not independent traits.}
\]

Expected counts:

| Gender | Blue | Brown | Green | Hazel |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Male | 329.97 | 286.09 | 142.82 | 153.12 |
| Female | 407.03 | 352.91 | 176.18 | 188.88 |

\[
\chi^2=12.67,\qquad df=3,\qquad p=0.005.
\]

Since \(p<0.01\), reject \(H_0\). There is sufficient evidence to warrant rejection of the claim that gender is independent of eye color.
