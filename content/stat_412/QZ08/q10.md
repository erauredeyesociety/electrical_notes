# Question 10

The accompanying table shows results of challenged referee calls in a major tennis tournament. Use a 0.05 significance level to test the claim that the gender of the tennis player is independent of whether a call is overturned.

| Gender | Successful challenge | Unsuccessful challenge |
|---|---:|---:|
| Men | 256 | 720 |
| Women | 464 | 962 |

## Answer

\[
H_0:\text{the gender of the tennis player is independent of whether a call is overturned.}
\]

\[
H_1:\text{the gender of the tennis player is not independent of whether a call is overturned.}
\]

Expected counts:

\[
\begin{array}{c|cc}
 & \text{Successful} & \text{Unsuccessful}\\ \hline
\text{Men} & 292.556 & 683.444\\
\text{Women} & 427.444 & 998.556
\end{array}
\]

\[
\chi^2=10.988,\qquad df=1,\qquad p=0.0009.
\]

Since \(p<0.05\), reject \(H_0\). There is sufficient evidence to warrant rejection of the claim that the gender of the tennis player is independent of whether a call is overturned.
