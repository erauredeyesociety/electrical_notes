\section*{STAT 412 QZ08 --- Question 2}

\subsection*{Solution}

For the 147 female heights,
\[
\bar{x}=161.6875,\qquad s=7.4838.
\]

Using the normal model based on the sample mean and sample standard deviation,
\[
z=\frac{x-\bar{x}}{s}.
\]
For example, the first cutoff gives
\[
z=\frac{156.05-161.6875}{7.4838}\approx -0.75,
\]
so the first class probability is about \(0.2256\). Multiplying each probability by \(n=147\) gives the expected frequencies.

\[
\begin{array}{lrrr}
\toprule
\text{Height class} & O & \text{Probability} & E\\
\midrule
<156.05 & 32 & 0.2256 & 33.17\\
156.05\text{--}162.15 & 40 & 0.2990 & 43.95\\
162.15\text{--}168.25 & 47 & 0.2851 & 41.91\\
>168.25 & 28 & 0.1903 & 27.97\\
\bottomrule
\end{array}
\]

\[
H_0:\text{each observed frequency equals its expected frequency.}
\]
\[
H_1:\text{at least one observed frequency differs from its expected frequency.}
\]

Because \(\mu\) and \(\sigma\) are estimated from the sample,
\[
df=4-1-2=1.
\]

The test statistic is
\[
\chi^2=\sum\frac{(O-E)^2}{E}
=\frac{(32-33.17)^2}{33.17}
+\frac{(40-43.95)^2}{43.95}
+\frac{(47-41.91)^2}{41.91}
+\frac{(28-27.97)^2}{27.97}
=1.02.
\]

\[
\chi^2=1.02,\qquad p=0.314.
\]

Since \(p>0.10\), fail to reject \(H_0\). The goodness-of-fit test does not give sufficient evidence against normality.

\subsection*{Final Answer}

\fbox{\begin{minipage}{0.94\linewidth}
Observed counts: \(32,40,47,28\). Expected counts: \(33.17,43.95,41.91,27.97\). \(\chi^2=1.02\), \(p=0.314\). Fail to reject \(H_0\).
\end{minipage}}
