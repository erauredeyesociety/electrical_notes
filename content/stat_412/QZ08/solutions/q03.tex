\section*{STAT 412 QZ08 --- Question 3}

\subsection*{Solution}

\[
H_0:\text{the observed frequencies agree with the expected proportions.}
\]
\[
H_1:\text{at least one observed frequency does not agree with the expected proportions.}
\]

Expected counts:
\[
95\left(\frac{2}{16},\frac{4}{16},\frac{5}{16},\frac{5}{16}\right)
=(11.875,23.750,29.688,29.688).
\]

The chi-square statistic is
\[
\chi^2=\sum\frac{(O-E)^2}{E}.
\]
Substituting the four observed counts,
\[
\chi^2=
\frac{(19-11.875)^2}{11.875}
+\frac{(18-23.750)^2}{23.750}
+\frac{(21-29.688)^2}{29.688}
+\frac{(37-29.688)^2}{29.688}
=10.011.
\]
With \(4\) categories,
\[
df=4-1=3.
\]

\[
\chi^2=10.011,\qquad df=3,\qquad p=0.0185.
\]

Since \(p<0.05\), reject \(H_0\).

\subsection*{Final Answer}

\fbox{\begin{minipage}{0.94\linewidth}
\(\chi^2=10.011\), \(p=0.0185\). Reject \(H_0\); there is sufficient evidence to reject the claim that the actual numbers fit the expected distribution.
\end{minipage}}
