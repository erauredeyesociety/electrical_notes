\section*{STAT 412 QZ08 --- Question 4}

\subsection*{Solution}

\[
H_0:\text{wins occur with equal frequency in the different post positions.}
\]
\[
H_1:\text{at least one post position has a different frequency of wins.}
\]

The observed counts sum to \(115\), so each expected count is \(11.5\).

The goodness-of-fit statistic is
\[
\chi^2=\sum\frac{(O-E)^2}{E}.
\]
For the ten post positions,
\[
\chi^2=
\frac{(19-11.5)^2}{11.5}
+\frac{(13-11.5)^2}{11.5}
+\cdots
+\frac{(10-11.5)^2}{11.5}
=12.043.
\]
There are \(10\) categories, so
\[
df=10-1=9.
\]

\[
\chi^2=12.043,\qquad df=9,\qquad p=0.2109.
\]

Since \(p>0.05\), fail to reject \(H_0\). Based on this test, bettors should not consider post position.

\subsection*{Final Answer}

\fbox{\begin{minipage}{0.94\linewidth}
\(\chi^2=12.043\), \(p=0.2109\). Fail to reject \(H_0\). No, bettors should not consider post position based on this result.
\end{minipage}}
