\section*{STAT 412 QZ08 --- Question 8}

\subsection*{Solution}

Let Brand A be population 1 and Brand B be population 2.

\[
H_0:\mu_1-\mu_2=0,\qquad H_1:\mu_1-\mu_2\ne0.
\]

With \(n_1=n_2=12\), \(\bar{x}_1=38100\), \(\bar{x}_2=40100\), \(s_1=5000\), and \(s_2=5900\), the pooled standard deviation is
\[
s_p=\sqrt{\frac{(n_1-1)s_1^2+(n_2-1)s_2^2}{n_1+n_2-2}}
=\sqrt{\frac{11(5000)^2+11(5900)^2}{22}}
=5468.55.
\]

\[
t=\frac{38100-40100}{5468.55\sqrt{1/12+1/12}}=-0.90.
\]
The degrees of freedom for the pooled test are
\[
df=n_1+n_2-2=22.
\]

The exact two-tailed P-value is \(0.380\), so the table range is
\[
0.3<P\text{-value}<0.4.
\]

Do not reject \(H_0\). There is insufficient evidence of a difference in average wear.

\subsection*{Final Answer}

\fbox{\begin{minipage}{0.94\linewidth}
\(H_0:\mu_1-\mu_2=0\), \(H_1:\mu_1-\mu_2\ne0\). \(t=-0.90\). \(0.3<P\text{-value}<0.4\). Do not reject \(H_0\).
\end{minipage}}
