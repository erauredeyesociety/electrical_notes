\section*{STAT 412 QZ08 --- Question 7}

\subsection*{Solution}

\[
H_0:\text{gender and eye color are independent traits.}
\]
\[
H_1:\text{gender and eye color are not independent traits.}
\]

Expected counts:
\[
E=\frac{(\text{row total})(\text{column total})}{\text{grand total}}.
\]
For example, the expected count for male students with blue eyes is
\[
E=\frac{912(737)}{2037}=329.97.
\]
\[
\begin{array}{c|cccc}
 & \text{Blue} & \text{Brown} & \text{Green} & \text{Hazel}\\ \hline
\text{Male} & 329.97 & 286.09 & 142.82 & 153.12\\
\text{Female} & 407.03 & 352.91 & 176.18 & 188.88
\end{array}
\]

The chi-square statistic adds the contribution from each cell:
\[
\chi^2=\sum\frac{(O-E)^2}{E}=12.67.
\]
The degrees of freedom are
\[
df=(2-1)(4-1)=3.
\]

\[
\chi^2=12.67,\qquad df=3,\qquad p=0.005.
\]

Since \(p<0.01\), reject \(H_0\).
