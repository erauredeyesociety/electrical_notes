# Question 1

Randomly selected birth records were categorized by day of the week. Use a 0.01 significance level to test the reasonable claim that births occur with equal frequency on the different days of the week. How might the apparent lower frequencies on Saturday and Sunday be explained?

| Day | Sun | Mon | Tues | Wed | Thurs | Fri | Sat |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Number of births | 36 | 61 | 65 | 55 | 57 | 58 | 43 |

## Answer

\[
H_0:\text{births occur with the same frequency on the different days of the week.}
\]

\[
H_1:\text{at least one day has a different frequency of births than the other days.}
\]

The total number of births is \(375\), so the expected count for each day is \(375/7=53.571\).

\[
\chi^2=11.941,\qquad df=6,\qquad p=0.0633.
\]

Since \(p>0.01\), fail to reject \(H_0\). There is insufficient evidence to warrant rejection of the claim that births occur with equal frequency on the different days of the week.

The lower Saturday/Sunday frequencies may be explained by induced or Caesarean-section births being scheduled during the week whenever possible.
