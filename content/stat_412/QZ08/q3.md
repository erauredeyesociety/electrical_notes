# Question 3

The table lists the number of games played in a yearly best-of-seven baseball championship series, along with the expected proportions for teams of equal abilities. Use a 0.05 significance level to test the claim that the actual numbers of games fit the distribution indicated by the expected proportions.

| Games played | 4 | 5 | 6 | 7 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Actual contests | 19 | 18 | 21 | 37 |
| Expected proportion | \(2/16\) | \(4/16\) | \(5/16\) | \(5/16\) |

## Answer

\[
H_0:\text{the observed frequencies agree with the expected proportions.}
\]

\[
H_1:\text{at least one observed frequency does not agree with the expected proportions.}
\]

Expected counts:

\[
95\left(\frac{2}{16},\frac{4}{16},\frac{5}{16},\frac{5}{16}\right)
=(11.875,\ 23.750,\ 29.688,\ 29.688).
\]

\[
\chi^2=10.011,\qquad df=3,\qquad p=0.0185.
\]

Since \(p<0.05\), reject \(H_0\). There is sufficient evidence to warrant rejection of the claim that the actual numbers of games fit the distribution indicated by the expected proportions.
