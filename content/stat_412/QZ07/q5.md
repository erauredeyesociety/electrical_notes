# Question 5

Aflatoxins produced by mold on peanut crops must be monitored. A sample of 49 batches of peanuts reveals levels of 24.74 ppm, on average, with a variance of 4.25 ppm\(^2\). Test the hypothesis that \(\sigma^2=4.2\) ppm against the alternative that \(\sigma^2\ne4.2\) ppm. Use a P-value in your conclusion.

Find the test statistic.

Round to two decimal places as needed.

Find the P-value.

Round to three decimal places as needed.

What is the appropriate decision for this test?

The test statistic \(\_\) significant at the 0.05 level. Based on the P-value, \(\_\ H_0\) and conclude that there \(\_\) sufficient evidence that \(\sigma^2\ne4.2\).

## Answer

Test statistic: \(\chi^2=48.57\).

P-value: \(0.900\).

The test statistic is not significant at the 0.05 level. Do not reject \(H_0\). There is not sufficient evidence that \(\sigma^2\ne4.2\).
