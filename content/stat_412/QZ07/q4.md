# Question 4

Without using the terms "null hypothesis" and "alternative hypothesis," identify the type I error and identify the type II error.

\[
H_0:\text{ The car is old.}
\]

\[
H_a:\text{ The car is new.}
\]

Choose the correct answer below.

A. Type I error and Type II error: Fail to reject the claim that the car is old when the car is new.

B. Type I error and Type II error: Reject the claim that the car is old when the car is old.

C. Type I error: Reject the claim that the car is old when the car is old. Type II error: Fail to reject the claim that the car is old when the car is new.

D. Type I error: Fail to reject the claim that the car is old when the car is new. Type II error: Reject the claim that the car is old when the car is old.

## Answer

Choice C.
