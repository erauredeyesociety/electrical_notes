# Question 2

A soft-drink machine at a steak house is regulated so that the amount of drink dispensed is approximately normally distributed with a mean of 190 milliliters and a standard deviation of 12 milliliters. The machine is checked periodically by taking a sample of 9 drinks and computing the average content. If \(\bar{x}\) falls in the interval \(181<\bar{x}<199\), the machine is thought to be operating satisfactorily; otherwise, the owner concludes that \(\mu\ne190\) milliliters. Complete parts (a) and (b) below.

The standard normal distribution table is available in `2.md`.

(a) Find the probability of committing a type I error when \(\mu=190\) milliliters.

Round to four decimal places as needed.

(b) Find the probability of committing a type II error when \(\mu=178\) milliliters.

Round to four decimal places as needed.

## Answer

(a) \(0.0244\)

(b) \(0.2266\)
