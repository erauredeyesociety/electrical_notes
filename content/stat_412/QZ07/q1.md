# Question 1

The average height of females in the freshman class of a certain college has historically been 162.2 centimeters with a standard deviation of 6.7 centimeters. Is there reason to believe that there has been a change in the average height if a random sample of 60 females in the present freshman class has an average height of 164.3 centimeters? Use a P-value in your conclusion. Assume the standard deviation remains the same.

State the null and alternative hypotheses.

\[
H_0:\mu\ \_\  \_\_
\]

\[
H_1:\mu\ \_\  \_\_
\]

Type integers or decimals. Do not round.

Find the test statistic.

Round to two decimal places as needed.

Find the P-value.

Round to three decimal places as needed.

What is the appropriate conclusion for this test?

\[
\_\ H_0.
\]

There \(\_\) sufficient evidence to conclude that there has been a change in the average height.

In general, \(H_0\) would be rejected for significance levels \(\_\) than the P-value.

## Answer

\[
H_0:\mu=162.2,\qquad H_1:\mu\ne162.2
\]

Test statistic: \(z=2.43\).

P-value: \(0.015\).

Reject \(H_0\). There is sufficient evidence to conclude that there has been a change in the average height. In general, \(H_0\) would be rejected for significance levels greater than the P-value.
