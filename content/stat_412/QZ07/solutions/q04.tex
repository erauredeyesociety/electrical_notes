\section*{STAT 412 QZ07 --- Question 4}

\subsection*{Problem}

Without using the terms ``null hypothesis'' and ``alternative hypothesis,'' identify the type I error and type II error.
\[
H_0:\text{ The car is old.}
\]
\[
H_a:\text{ The car is new.}
\]

\subsection*{Solution}

A type I error rejects a true $H_0$, so it means rejecting the claim that the car is old when the car is old.

A type II error fails to reject a false $H_0$, so it means failing to reject the claim that the car is old when the car is new.

\subsection*{Final Answer}

\fbox{Choice C.}
