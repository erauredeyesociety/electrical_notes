\section*{STAT 412 QZ07 --- Question 5}

\subsection*{Problem}

A sample of 49 batches of peanuts has mean aflatoxin level 24.74 ppm and variance 4.25 ppm$^2$. Test
\[
H_0:\sigma^2=4.2
\]
against
\[
H_a:\sigma^2\ne4.2.
\]
Use a P-value in the conclusion.

\subsection*{Solution}

For a variance test,
\[
\chi^2=\frac{(n-1)s^2}{\sigma_0^2}.
\]

Substitute $n=49$, $s^2=4.25$, and $\sigma_0^2=4.2$:
\[
\chi^2=\frac{48(4.25)}{4.2}=48.57.
\]

With $df=48$, the two-tailed P-value is
\[
P\approx 0.900.
\]

Since $0.900>0.05$, the result is not significant at the 0.05 level.

\subsection*{Final Answer}

\fbox{\begin{minipage}{0.94\linewidth}
$\chi^2=48.57$; P-value $=0.900$. The test statistic is not significant at the 0.05 level. Do not reject $H_0$; there is not sufficient evidence that $\sigma^2\ne4.2$.
\end{minipage}}
