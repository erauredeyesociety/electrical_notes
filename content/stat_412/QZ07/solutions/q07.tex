\section*{STAT 412 QZ07 --- Question 7}

\subsection*{Problem}

In a study of 799 randomly selected medical malpractice lawsuits, 509 were dropped or dismissed. Test the claim that most medical malpractice lawsuits are dropped or dismissed. Use the P-value method and the normal distribution as an approximation to the binomial distribution.

\subsection*{Solution}

The word ``most'' means more than half, so
\[
H_0:p=0.5,\qquad H_a:p>0.5.
\]
This is choice B.

The sample proportion is
\[
\widehat{p}=\frac{509}{799}=0.6370.
\]

The one-proportion test statistic is
\[
z=\frac{\widehat{p}-p_0}{\sqrt{p_0(1-p_0)/n}}
=\frac{0.6370-0.5}{\sqrt{0.5(0.5)/799}}
=7.75.
\]

This is a right-tailed test:
\[
P=P(Z\ge 7.75)\approx 0.000.
\]

Since the P-value is less than or equal to the significance level, reject $H_0$.

\subsection*{Final Answer}

\fbox{\begin{minipage}{0.94\linewidth}
Hypotheses: choice B. $z=7.75$; P-value $=0.000$. Conclusion about $H_0$: choice B. Final conclusion: choice D.
\end{minipage}}
