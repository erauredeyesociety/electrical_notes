\section*{STAT 412 QZ07 --- Question 3}

\subsection*{Problem}

Illegal drug use among 12- to 17-year-olds is currently reported as 9.7\%. In a random sample of 1,056 students at a large high school, 98 report using illegal drugs. Use $\alpha=0.05$ to test the principal's claim that illegal drug use in her school is below the current national average.

\subsection*{Solution}

The claim is ``below,'' so use a left-tailed one-proportion test:
\[
H_0:p=0.097,\qquad H_a:p<0.097.
\]

The sample proportion is
\[
\widehat{p}=\frac{98}{1056}=0.0928.
\]

The test statistic is
\[
z=\frac{\widehat{p}-p_0}{\sqrt{p_0(1-p_0)/n}}
=\frac{0.0928-0.097}{\sqrt{0.097(0.903)/1056}}
=-0.46.
\]

The left-tailed P-value is
\[
P=P(Z\le -0.46)\approx 0.3225.
\]

Since $0.3225>0.05$, do not reject $H_0$.

\subsection*{Final Answer}

\fbox{\begin{minipage}{0.94\linewidth}
Hypotheses: choice B. $z=-0.46$, P-value $=0.3225$. Conclusion: choice C; do not reject $H_0$. There is insufficient evidence to support the principal's claim.
\end{minipage}}
