# Question 20

Decide whether the statement makes sense (or is clearly true) or does not make sense (or is clearly false).

In hypothesis tests, if the significance level is 0.01, then the P-value is also 0.01.

Choose the correct answer.

A. The statement makes sense. The significance level and the P-value represent the same component of the hypothesis test.

B. The statement does not make sense. The significance level and the P-value represent different components of the hypothesis test, and are generally not the same.

C. The statement does not make sense. The P-value is the probability of finding a sample statistic less extreme than the observed statistic given that the null hypothesis is true.

D. The statement does not make sense. The P-value is the probability of finding a sample statistic at least as extreme as the observed statistic given that the null hypothesis is false.
