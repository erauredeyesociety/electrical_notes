# Question 1

Suppose that an allergist wishes to test the hypothesis that at least 31% of the public is allergic to some cheese products. Explain how the allergist could commit the following types of errors.

(a) A type I error.

(b) A type II error.

## Answers

Use the platform wording for the claim being tested: **31% or more** means the allergist is concluding the tested claim is true, and **less than 31%** means the allergist is concluding the tested claim is false.

(a) Type I error: The allergist could conclude that **31% or more** of the public are allergic when, in fact, **less than 31%** are allergic.

(b) Type II error: The allergist could conclude that **less than 31%** of the public are allergic when, in fact, **31% or more** are allergic.
