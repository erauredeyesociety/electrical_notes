# Question 12

It is claimed that automobiles are driven on average more than 18,000 kilometers per year. To test this claim, 110 randomly selected automobile owners are asked to keep a record of the kilometers they travel. Would you agree with this claim if the random sample showed an average of 18,330 kilometers and a standard deviation of 3700 kilometers? Use a P-value in your conclusion.

The table of critical values of the t-distribution is available in `12.md`.

Identify the null and alternative hypotheses.

Choices:

- A. \(H_0:\mu \ne 18000,\ H_a:\mu=18000\)
- B. \(H_0:\mu=18000,\ H_a:\mu<18000\)
- C. \(H_0:\mu<18000,\ H_a:\mu=18000\)
- D. \(H_0:\mu=18000,\ H_a:\mu>18000\)
- E. \(H_0:\mu>18000,\ H_a:\mu=18000\)
- F. \(H_0:\mu>18000,\ H_a:\mu=18000\)

What is the appropriate conclusion for this test?

\[
\_\ H_0
\]

and conclude that there \(\_\) sufficient evidence to agree with the claim that automobiles are driven on average more than 18,000 kilometers per year.

In general, \(H_0\) would be rejected for significance levels \(\_\) than the P-value.

## Answer

The screenshot reads **18,330** kilometers, not 18,230.

- Hypotheses: choice **D**, \(H_0:\mu=18000\), \(H_a:\mu>18000\).
- Test statistic:
  \[
  t=\frac{18330-18000}{3700/\sqrt{110}}\approx0.93542.
  \]
- Rounded test statistic: **0.94**.
- Exact right-tail P-value with \(df=109\): about **0.1758**, so enter **0.176** if three decimals are required.
- Table range: **0.15 < P-value < 0.20**.
- Conclusion fill-ins: **Do not reject**; **is not**; **greater**.
