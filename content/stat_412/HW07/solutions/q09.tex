\section*{STAT 412 HW07 --- Question 9}

\subsection*{Problem}

\begin{quote}
A marketing expert for a pasta-making company believes that 40\% of pasta lovers prefer lasagna. If 12 out of 20 pasta lovers choose lasagna over other pastas, what can be concluded about the expert's claim? Use a 0.05 level of significance.
\end{quote}

The conclusion choices include:
\begin{enumerate}
\item[A.] Reject $H_0$ and conclude that there is sufficient evidence that the percentage is not 40\%.
\item[B.] Do not reject $H_0$ and conclude that there is not sufficient evidence that the percentage is not 40\%.
\item[C.] Do not reject $H_0$ and conclude that there is sufficient evidence that the percentage is not 40\%.
\item[D.] Reject $H_0$ and conclude that there is not sufficient evidence that the percentage is not 40\%.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection*{Solution}

The expert's claim is an equality claim about the proportion:
\[
H_0:p=0.4.
\]
Because the question asks what can be concluded about the claim, without saying specifically above or below, use a two-sided alternative:
\[
H_a:p\ne0.4.
\]
This is choice C.

Let $X$ be the number of pasta lovers in the sample who choose lasagna. Under the null hypothesis,
\[
X\sim\operatorname{Binomial}(n=20,p=0.4).
\]
So the platform entries for the binomial model are $p=0.4$ and $n=20$.

The observed value is $x=12$. MyStatLab's feedback uses the doubled one-tail exact-binomial rule for a two-sided test. Since $x=12$ is greater than $np_0=8$,
\[
P\text{-value}=2P(X\ge12)
=2(0.056526367)
\approx 0.1131.
\]

Since $0.1131>0.05$, do not reject $H_0$. The correct conclusion choice is B.

\subsection*{Final Answer}

\fbox{\begin{minipage}{0.94\linewidth}
Choice C: $H_0:p=0.4$; $H_a:p\ne0.4$. Test statistic/model: $X\sim\operatorname{Binomial}(n=20,p=0.4)$. P-value $\approx0.1131$; do not reject $H_0$ at $\alpha=0.05$. Conclusion choice B.
\end{minipage}}
