\section*{STAT 412 HW07 --- Question 9}

\subsection*{Problem}

\begin{quote}
A marketing expert for a pasta-making company believes that 40\% of pasta lovers prefer lasagna. If 12 out of 20 pasta lovers choose lasagna over other pastas, what can be concluded about the expert's claim? Use a 0.08 level of significance.
\end{quote}

\subsection*{Solution}

The expert's claim is an equality claim about the proportion:
\[
H_0:p=0.4.
\]
Because the question asks what can be concluded about the claim, without saying specifically above or below, use a two-sided alternative:
\[
H_a:p\ne0.4.
\]
This is choice C.

Let $X$ be the number of pasta lovers in the sample who choose lasagna. Under the null hypothesis,
\[
X\sim\operatorname{Binomial}(n=20,p=0.4).
\]
So the platform entries for the binomial model are $p=0.4$ and $n=20$.

The observed value is $x=12$. For the two-sided exact binomial P-value:
\[
P(X\ge12)+P(X\le4)
\approx 0.0565+0.0510
=0.1075.
\]

Since $0.1075>0.08$, do not reject $H_0$.

\subsection*{Final Answer}

\fbox{\begin{minipage}{0.94\linewidth}
Choice C: $H_0:p=0.4$; $H_a:p\ne0.4$. Test statistic/model: $X\sim\operatorname{Binomial}(n=20,p=0.4)$. P-value $\approx0.1075$; do not reject $H_0$.
\end{minipage}}
