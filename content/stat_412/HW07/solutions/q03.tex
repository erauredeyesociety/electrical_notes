\section*{STAT 412 HW07 --- Question 3}

\subsection*{Problem}

\begin{quote}
A soft-drink machine at a steak house is regulated so that the amount of drink dispensed is approximately normally distributed with a mean of 210 milliliters and a standard deviation of 15 milliliters. A sample of 9 drinks is taken. If $\bar X$ falls in the interval $201<\bar X<219$, the machine is thought to be operating satisfactorily; otherwise, the owner concludes that $\mu\ne210$.
\end{quote}

\subsection*{Solution}

The standard deviation of the sample mean is
\[
\sigma_{\bar X}=\frac{15}{\sqrt{9}}=5.
\]

\textbf{Part (a).} A type I error occurs when $\mu=210$ but the sample mean falls outside the acceptable interval. The cutoffs are
\[
z=\frac{201-210}{5}=-1.80,\qquad z=\frac{219-210}{5}=1.80.
\]
Therefore
\[
\alpha=P(Z<-1.80)+P(Z>1.80)=2(0.0359)=0.0718.
\]

\textbf{Part (b).} A type II error when $\mu=195$ means failing to reject, so $201<\bar X<219$. Standardize using $\mu=195$:
\[
z_1=\frac{201-195}{5}=1.20,\qquad z_2=\frac{219-195}{5}=4.80.
\]
Thus
\[
\beta=P(1.20<Z<4.80)\approx 0.1151.
\]

\subsection*{Final Answer}

\fbox{\begin{minipage}{0.94\linewidth}
(a) $\alpha=0.0718$. (b) When $\mu=195$, $\beta=0.1151$.
\end{minipage}}
