\section*{STAT 412 HW07 --- Question 13}

\subsection*{Problem}

\begin{quote}
One research study of illegal drug use among 12- to 17-year-olds reported a decrease in use from 11.4\% in 1997 to 9.7\% now. Suppose a survey in a large high school reveals that, in a random sample of 1,050 students, 99 report using illegal drugs. Use a 0.05 significance level to test the principal's claim that illegal drug use in her school is below the current national average.
\end{quote}

The conclusion choices include:
\begin{enumerate}
\item[A.] Do not reject $H_0$; sufficient evidence to support the claim.
\item[B.] Reject $H_0$; insufficient evidence to support the claim.
\item[C.] Do not reject $H_0$; insufficient evidence to support the claim.
\item[D.] Reject $H_0$; sufficient evidence to support the claim.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection*{Solution}

The principal's claim is ``below the current national average,'' so this is a left-tailed test:
\[
H_0:p=0.097,\qquad H_a:p<0.097.
\]
This is choice D.

The sample proportion is
\[
\hat{p}=\frac{99}{1050}\approx 0.0942857.
\]

Using the exact sample proportion,
\[
z=\frac{\hat{p}-p_0}{\sqrt{p_0(1-p_0)/n}}
  =\frac{0.0942857-0.097}{\sqrt{0.097(0.903)/1050}}
  \approx -0.29718,
\]
which rounds to $-0.30$.

The platform-confirmed test statistic is $z=-0.30$.

The P-value for the found statistic is
\[
P(Z<-0.30)=0.3821.
\]

Since $0.3821>0.05$, do not reject $H_0$. The correct conclusion choice is C.

\subsection*{Final Answer}

\fbox{\begin{minipage}{0.94\linewidth}
Choice D: $H_0:p=0.097$; $H_a:p<0.097$. $z=-0.30$; P-value $=0.3821$. Do not reject $H_0$; conclusion choice C.
\end{minipage}}
