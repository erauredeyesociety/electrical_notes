\section*{STAT 412 HW07 --- Question 17}

\subsection*{Problem}

\begin{quote}
Find the P-value that corresponds to the given standard score, and determine whether to reject the null hypothesis at the 0.10 significance level. Is the alternative hypothesis supported?

z = -2.2 for H0: mu = 23 centimeters and Ha: mu < 23 centimeters.

Find the P-value. Round to four decimal places.
\end{quote}

\subsection*{Solution}

The alternative is mu<23, so this is left-tailed. P-value=P(Z<-2.2)=0.0139, which is less than 0.10.

\subsection*{Final Answer}

\fbox{\begin{minipage}{0.94\linewidth}
P-value = 0.0139; reject H0 at alpha=0.10; the alternative is supported.
\end{minipage}}
