\section*{STAT 412 HW07 --- Question 16}

\subsection*{Problem}

\begin{quote}
An annual survey of first-year college students asks 280,000 students about their attitudes on a variety of subjects. According to a recent survey, 52\% of first-year students believe that abortion should be legal. Use a 0.05 significance level to test the claim that over half of all first-year students believe that abortion should be legal.

Formulate the null and alternative hypotheses.

Choices: A. H0:p<0.5, Ha:p=0.5. B. H0:p=0.5, Ha:p<0.5. C. H0:p=0.5, Ha:p != 0.5. D. H0:p=0.5, Ha:p>0.5.
\end{quote}

\subsection*{Solution}

The claim is over half, so Ha:p>0.5. z=(0.52-0.50)/sqrt(0.5(0.5)/280000)=21.1660.

\subsection*{Final Answer}

\fbox{\begin{minipage}{0.94\linewidth}
Choice D: H0: p=0.5; Ha: p>0.5. z=21.17; P-value essentially 0, so reject H0.
\end{minipage}}
