\section*{STAT 412 HW07 --- Question 15}

\subsection*{Problem}

\begin{quote}
According to a study in a previous year, 54.0\% of households nationwide used natural gas for heating during a year. Recently, a survey of 3,500 randomly selected households showed that 55.0\% used natural gas. Use a 0.05 significance level to test the claim that the 54.0\% national rate has changed.

Formulate the null and alternative hypotheses.

Choices: A. H0:p=0.54, Ha:p>0.54. B. H0:p=0.54, Ha:p<0.54. C. H0:p=0.54, Ha:p != 0.54. D. H0:p != 0.54, Ha:p=0.54.
\end{quote}

\subsection*{Solution}

The claim says the rate has changed, so use a two-sided alternative. z=(0.55-0.54)/sqrt(0.54(0.46)/3500)=1.1870. The two-sided P-value is about 0.235, which is greater than 0.05. Therefore the visible data do not reject H0. If the platform says to reject H0, then a later screenshot likely has a different sample percentage or asks a different decision.

\subsection*{Final Answer}

\fbox{\begin{minipage}{0.94\linewidth}
Choice C: H0: p=0.54; Ha: p != 0.54. z=1.19; P-value about 0.235, so do not reject H0 from the visible 55.0\% data.
\end{minipage}}
