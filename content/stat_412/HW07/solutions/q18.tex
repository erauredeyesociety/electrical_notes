\section*{STAT 412 HW07 --- Question 18}

\subsection*{Problem}

\begin{quote}
Find the P-value that corresponds to the given standard score, and determine whether to reject the null hypothesis at the 0.01 significance level. Is the alternative hypothesis supported?

$z=2.6$ for $H_0:\mu=149$ pounds and $H_a:\mu\ne149$ pounds.
\end{quote}

\subsection*{Solution}

The alternative is two-sided, so use both tails:
\[
P\text{-value}=2P(Z>2.6).
\]

From the standard normal distribution,
\[
P(Z>2.6)\approx 0.00466.
\]
Therefore,
\[
P\text{-value}\approx 2(0.00466)=0.0093.
\]

Since $0.0093\le0.01$, reject $H_0$. The alternative hypothesis is supported.

\subsection*{Final Answer}

\fbox{\begin{minipage}{0.94\linewidth}
P-value $=0.0093$. Correct conclusion choice: B; reject $H_0$ and the alternative hypothesis is supported.
\end{minipage}}
