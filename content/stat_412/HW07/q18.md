# Question 18

Find the P-value that corresponds to the given standard score, and determine whether to reject the null hypothesis at the 0.01 significance level. Is the alternative hypothesis supported?

\[
z=2.6,\qquad H_0:\mu=149 \text{ pounds},\qquad H_a:\mu\ne149 \text{ pounds}.
\]

Find the P-value. Round to four decimal places.

## Cleaned / confirmed parts

Because \(H_a\) is two-sided,
\[
\text{P-value}=2P(Z>2.6)\approx 0.0093.
\]

Since \(0.0093\le 0.01\), reject \(H_0\). The alternative hypothesis is supported.

Correct conclusion choice: **B** — the P-value is less than or equal to the significance level; reject \(H_0\); the alternative hypothesis is supported.
