# Question 15

According to a study in a previous year, 54.0% of households nationwide used natural gas for heating during a year. Recently, a survey of 3,500 randomly selected households showed that 55.0% used natural gas. Use a 0.05 significance level to test the claim that the 54.0% national rate has changed.

Formulate the null and alternative hypotheses.

Choices: A. H0:p=0.54, Ha:p>0.54. B. H0:p=0.54, Ha:p<0.54. C. H0:p=0.54, Ha:p != 0.54. D. H0:p != 0.54, Ha:p=0.54.

Note: With the visible screenshot values p-hat = 0.55, p0 = 0.54, n = 3500, and alpha = 0.05, the two-tailed P-value is about 0.235, so these visible data do **not** reject H0. If the platform says to reject H0, a later screenshot likely has a different sample percentage or asks a different decision.
