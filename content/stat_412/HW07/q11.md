# Question 11

In the American Heart Association journal *Hypertension*, researchers report that individuals who practice Transcendental Meditation (TM) lower their blood pressure significantly. If a random sample of 240 male TM practitioners meditate for 10.5 hours per week with a standard deviation of 2.22 hours, does that suggest that, on average, men who use TM meditate more than 10 hours per week? Quote a P-value in your conclusion.

State the null and alternative hypotheses.

## Answer

- \(H_0:\mu=10\)
- \(H_a:\mu>10\)
- \(t=(10.5-10)/(2.22/\sqrt{240})=3.49\)
- Right-tail P-value \(\approx0.0003\); enter **0.000** if three decimals are required.
- Reject \(H_0\). There is sufficient evidence that male TM practitioners meditate more than 10 hours per week on average.
- In general, \(H_0\) would be rejected for significance levels **greater** than the P-value.
