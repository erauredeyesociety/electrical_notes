# Question 6

The average height of females in the freshman class of a certain college has historically been 162.8 centimeters with a standard deviation of 6.8 centimeters. Is there reason to believe that there has been a change in the average height if a random sample of 48 females in the present freshman class has an average height of 165.8 centimeters? Use a P-value in your conclusion. Assume the standard deviation remains the same.

State the null and alternative hypotheses.

## Cleaned / confirmed parts

- Because the question asks whether there has been a **change**, this is a two-tailed test.
- Platform-confirmed hypotheses:
  - \(H_0: \mu = 162.8\)
  - \(H_1: \mu \ne 162.8\)
- Platform-confirmed test statistic: \(z = 3.06\).
- The P-value prompt says **round to three decimal places**.
  - Exact/tabled two-tailed P-value: \(0.0022389\)
  - Four-decimal form: \(0.0022\)
  - Correct three-decimal platform entry: **0.002**

Conclusion: reject \(H_0\). There is sufficient evidence that the mean height has changed.
