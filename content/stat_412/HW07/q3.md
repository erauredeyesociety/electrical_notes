# Question 3

A soft-drink machine at a steak house is regulated so that the amount of drink dispensed is approximately normally distributed with a mean of 210 milliliters and a standard deviation of 15 milliliters. The machine is checked periodically by taking a sample of 9 drinks and computing the average content. If \(\bar{X}\) falls in the interval \(201<\bar{X}<219\), the machine is thought to be operating satisfactorily; otherwise, the owner concludes that \(\mu\ne210\) milliliters. Complete parts (a) and (b).

## Cleaned / confirmed parts

Here
\[
\sigma_{\bar{X}}=\frac{15}{\sqrt{9}}=5.
\]

(a) Type I error when \(\mu=210\):

Reject \(H_0\) when \(\bar{X}\le201\) or \(\bar{X}\ge219\). Under \(\mu=210\),
\[
z=\frac{201-210}{5}=-1.80,\qquad z=\frac{219-210}{5}=1.80.
\]

Platform-confirmed:

- \(\alpha=0.0718\)

(b) Type II error when \(\mu=195\):

Fail to reject when \(201<\bar{X}<219\). Under \(\mu=195\),
\[
\beta=P(201<\bar{X}<219)
  =P\left(\frac{201-195}{5}<Z<\frac{219-195}{5}\right)
  =P(1.20<Z<4.80).
\]

Thus
\[
\beta\approx 0.1151.
\]
