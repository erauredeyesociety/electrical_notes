# Question 10

Aflatoxins produced by mold on peanut crops must be monitored. A sample of 49 batches of peanuts reveals levels of 24.36 ppm, on average, with a variance of 4.35 ppm. Test the hypothesis that \(\sigma^2=4.1\) ppm against the alternative that \(\sigma^2\ne4.1\) ppm. Use a P-value in your conclusion.

Find the test statistic. Round to two decimal places.

Later visible parts ask for the P-value and the conclusion.

## Cleaned / confirmed parts

For a variance test,
\[
\chi^2=\frac{(n-1)s^2}{\sigma_0^2}
      =\frac{48(4.35)}{4.1}
      \approx 50.93.
\]

Platform-confirmed values:

- Test statistic: **50.93**
- P-value, rounded to three decimals: **0.718**

Because \(0.718>0.05\):

- The test statistic **is not** significant at the 0.05 level.
- Based on the P-value, **fail to reject** \(H_0\).
- There **is not** sufficient evidence that \(\sigma^2\ne4.1\).
