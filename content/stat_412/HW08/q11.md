# Question 11

Observed homicide counts by season:

| Season | Frequency |
|---|---:|
| Spring | 304 |
| Summer | 316 |
| Fall | 296 |
| Winter | 286 |

Test whether homicides are uniformly distributed across the four seasons at \(\alpha=0.01\).

## Solution

- \(H_0\): homicides are uniformly distributed across seasons.
- \(H_1\): homicides are not uniformly distributed across seasons.
- Total \(n=1202\); expected count per season \(=1202/4=300.5\).
- Chi-square statistic: \(\chi^2=1.607\).
- P-value: \(0.658\).
- Conclusion: do not reject \(H_0\). There is not sufficient evidence that homicides are distributed differently by season.
