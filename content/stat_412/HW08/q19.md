# Question 19

Observed births by day of the week:

| Day | Number of births |
|---|---:|
| Sunday | 44 |
| Monday | 60 |
| Tuesday | 57 |
| Wednesday | 59 |
| Thursday | 57 |
| Friday | 55 |
| Saturday | 36 |

Test whether births occur with equal frequency on the days of the week.

## Solution

- \(H_0\): births occur with equal frequency on the days of the week.
- \(H_1\): births do not occur with equal frequency on the days of the week.
- Total \(n=368\); expected count per day \(=368/7=52.5714\).
- Test statistic: \(\chi^2=9.315\).
- P-value: \(0.1566\) rounded to four decimals.
- Conclusion at \(\alpha=0.01\): do not reject \(H_0\). There is not sufficient evidence that births are unevenly distributed across the days of the week.

## Explanation choice

The result does **not** support a nonuniform distribution at the 1% significance level. If the platform asks for the practical explanation, the relevant context is that scheduled induced births and Caesarean-section births tend to be planned for weekdays when possible.



How might the apparent lower frequencies on Saturday and Sunday be​ explained?

Answer: **A.** Induced or Caesarean-section births are scheduled during the week whenever possible.
