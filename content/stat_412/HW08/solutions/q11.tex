\section*{HW08 Question 11}

\textbf{Problem.} Homicide counts by season are Spring 304, Summer 316, Fall 296, Winter 286. Test whether homicides are uniformly distributed across seasons at \(\alpha=0.01\).

\textbf{Solution.}
\[
H_0:\text{uniform by season},\qquad H_1:\text{not uniform by season}.
\]
Total:
\[
n=304+316+296+286=1202.
\]
Expected count in each season:
\[
E=\frac{1202}{4}=300.5.
\]
\[
\chi^2=\sum\frac{(O-E)^2}{E}=1.61,\qquad df=4-1=3.
\]
The P-value is \(0.658\).

\[
\boxed{\text{Do not reject }H_0.}
\]
There is not sufficient evidence that homicides are distributed differently across seasons.
