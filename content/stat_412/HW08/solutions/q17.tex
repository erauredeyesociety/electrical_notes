\section*{HW08 Question 17}

\textbf{Problem.} Homicide counts by month are \(39,31,45,39,47,50,48,50,50,42,38,38\). Test whether homicides are uniformly distributed across the months.

\textbf{Solution.}
\[
H_0:\text{uniform by month},\qquad H_1:\text{not uniform by month}.
\]
\[
n=517,\qquad E=\frac{517}{12}=43.0833.
\]
\[
\chi^2=\sum\frac{(O-E)^2}{E}=9.72,\qquad df=12-1=11.
\]
The P-value is \(0.555\).

\[
\boxed{\text{Do not reject }H_0.}
\]
There is not sufficient evidence that homicides occur more often in some months than others.
