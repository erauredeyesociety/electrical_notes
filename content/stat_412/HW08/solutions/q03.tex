\section*{HW08 Question 3}

\textbf{Problem.} Film times are:

Company 1: \(105,86,95,99,84\). Company 2: \(89,156,103,141,98,79,112\). Test whether company 2 exceeds company 1 by 10 minutes against the alternative that the difference is less than 10. Use unequal variances and \(\alpha=0.05\).

\textbf{Solution.}
\[
H_0:\mu_1-\mu_2=-10,\qquad H_1:\mu_1-\mu_2>-10.
\]
This is the same claim as \(\mu_2-\mu_1=10\) versus \(\mu_2-\mu_1<10\), but it matches the platform's displayed \(\mu_1-\mu_2\) dropdown format.
From the data,
\[
\bar{x}_1=93.80,\quad s_1=8.81,\qquad \bar{x}_2=111.14,\quad s_2=27.89.
\]
\[
t=\frac{(\bar{x}_1-\bar{x}_2)-(-10)}{\sqrt{s_1^2/5+s_2^2/7}}
=\frac{(93.80-111.14)+10}{\sqrt{8.81^2/5+27.89^2/7}}
=-0.65.
\]
Welch's degrees of freedom are \(df\approx7.57\). The right-tail P-value is \(0.733\).

\[
\boxed{\text{Do not reject }H_0. \text{ There is not enough evidence that the difference is less than 10.}}
\]
