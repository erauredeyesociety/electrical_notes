\section*{HW08 Question 9}

\textbf{Problem.} Independent samples of size 400 are selected from two populations. Sample 1 has \(\bar{x}_1=5315,\ s_1=157\). Sample 2 has \(\bar{x}_2=5275,\ s_2=199\). Find a 95\% confidence interval for \(\mu_1-\mu_2\), rounded to one decimal place.

\textbf{Solution.}
\[
\bar{x}_1-\bar{x}_2=5315-5275=40.
\]
\[
SE=\sqrt{\frac{157^2}{400}+\frac{199^2}{400}}=12.6738.
\]
Using \(z_{0.025}=1.96\),
\[
40\pm1.96(12.6738)=40\pm24.8406.
\]
\[
\boxed{(15.2,\ 64.8)}.
\]
We are 95\% confident that \(\mu_1-\mu_2\) is between 15.2 and 64.8.
