\section*{HW08 Question 16}

\textbf{Problem.} Tennis challenge results:
\[
\begin{array}{c|cc}
 & \text{Successful} & \text{Unsuccessful}\\ \hline
\text{Men} & 246 & 556\\
\text{Women} & 475 & 680
\end{array}
\]

\textbf{Solution.}
\[
H_0:\text{gender and challenge result are independent},\qquad
H_1:\text{they are not independent}.
\]
\[
\chi^2=\sum\frac{(O-E)^2}{E}=22.22,\qquad df=1.
\]
The P-value is approximately \(0.000\), so reject \(H_0\).

\[
\boxed{\text{Gender and challenge result are not independent.}}
\]
