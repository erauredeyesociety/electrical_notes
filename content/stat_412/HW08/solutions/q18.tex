\section*{HW08 Question 18}

\textbf{Problem.} For best-of-seven contests, actual counts for 4, 5, 6, and 7 games are \(20,19,22,36\). Expected proportions are \(0.125,0.250,0.3125,0.3125\).

\textbf{Solution.}
\[
H_0:\text{actual distribution fits the expected proportions},\qquad
H_1:\text{it does not fit}.
\]
Total contests:
\[
n=20+19+22+36=97.
\]
Expected counts:
\[
97(0.125,0.250,0.3125,0.3125)=(12.125,24.250,30.313,30.313).
\]
\[
\chi^2=\sum\frac{(O-E)^2}{E}=9.60,\qquad df=4-1=3.
\]
The P-value is \(0.022\).

\[
\boxed{\text{At }\alpha=0.05,\ \text{reject }H_0.}
\]
The actual distribution does not fit the expected proportions.
