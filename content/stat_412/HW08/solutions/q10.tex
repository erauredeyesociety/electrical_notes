\section*{HW08 Question 10}

\textbf{Problem.} Observed food-delivery day counts are Sunday 42, Monday 17, Tuesday 25, Wednesday 49, Thursday 41, Friday 165, Saturday 150. At \(\alpha=0.01\), test the claim that the distribution differs from the expected distribution.

\textbf{Known setup.}
\[
H_0:\text{the distribution is as shown in the pie chart.}
\]
\[
H_a:\text{the distribution differs from the expected distribution.}
\]
The claim is \(H_a\).

\textbf{Solution.} Using the pie-chart percentages in day order Sunday through Saturday,
\[
(0.12,0.02,0.03,0.14,0.10,0.31,0.28),
\]
which gives expected counts
\[
(58.680,9.780,14.670,68.460,48.900,151.590,136.920).
\]
\[
\chi^2=\sum\frac{(O_i-E_i)^2}{E_i}=26.589,\qquad df=7-1=6.
\]
\[
p=P(\chi^2_6\ge26.589)\approx0.000173.
\]
Rounded to three decimals, \(p=0.000\). Since \(p<0.01\), reject \(H_0\). There is enough evidence to support the claim that the distribution differs from the expected distribution.

\[
\boxed{\chi^2=26.589,\quad p=0.000,\quad \text{reject }H_0.}
\]
