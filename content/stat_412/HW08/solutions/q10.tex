\textbf{Problem.} Observed food-delivery day counts are Sunday 42, Monday 17, Tuesday 25, Wednesday 49, Thursday 41, Friday 165, Saturday 150. At \(\alpha=0.01\), test whether the distribution differs from the distribution shown in a pie chart.

\textbf{Known setup.}
\[
H_0:\text{the distribution is the same as the pie-chart distribution.}
\]
\[
H_a:\text{the distribution is different from the pie-chart distribution.}
\]
The claim is \(H_a\).

\textbf{Pending data.} The expected proportions from the pie chart are not visible. Once those are supplied, compute \(E_i=489p_i\) and
\[
\chi^2=\sum\frac{(O_i-E_i)^2}{E_i}.
\]
