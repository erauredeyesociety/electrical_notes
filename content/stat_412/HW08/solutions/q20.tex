\section*{HW08 Question 20}

\textbf{Problem.} Survey results for high school students at least 16 years old are used to test the claim of independence between texting while driving and driving when drinking alcohol. Use \(\alpha=0.05\).

\textbf{Known setup.}
\[
H_0:\text{texting while driving and driving after drinking alcohol are independent.}
\]
\[
H_1:\text{texting while driving and driving after drinking alcohol are not independent.}
\]

\textbf{Observed counts.} Let the columns be driving when drinking alcohol: yes/no.
\[
\begin{array}{c|cc|c}
 & \text{Yes} & \text{No} & \text{Total}\\ \hline
\text{Texted while driving} & 725 & 3024 & 3749\\
\text{No texting while driving} & 159 & 4563 & 4722\\ \hline
\text{Total} & 884 & 7587 & 8471
\end{array}
\]

\textbf{Expected counts.} Compute expected counts with
\[
E_{ij}=\frac{(\text{row total})(\text{column total})}{\text{grand total}}
\]
which gives
\[
\begin{array}{c|cc}
 & \text{Yes} & \text{No}\\ \hline
\text{Texted while driving} & 391.231 & 3357.769\\
\text{No texting while driving} & 492.769 & 4229.231
\end{array}
\]

\textbf{Test statistic and conclusion.}
\[
\chi^2=\sum\frac{(O-E)^2}{E}=570.3387,\qquad df=1.
\]
\[
p=P(\chi^2_1\ge570.3387)\approx4.74\times10^{-126}.
\]
For platform entry, this rounds to \(0.000\) to three decimal places or \(0.0000\) to four decimal places.
Since \(p<0.05\), reject \(H_0\). Texting while driving and driving after drinking alcohol are not independent.

\[
\boxed{\chi^2=570.34,\quad df=1,\quad p\approx0;\ \text{reject }H_0.}
\]
\[
\boxed{\text{Platform entries: }\chi^2=570.34,\quad P\text{-value}=0.000.}
\]
