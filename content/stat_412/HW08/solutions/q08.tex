\section*{HW08 Question 8}

\textbf{Problem.} A study compares 1,502 first-grade students in classrooms using educational software with 1,115 first-grade students in classrooms not using the software. Identify the parameter of interest. Let group 1 be software users and group 2 be non-users.

\textbf{Solution.}

The parameter compares the population mean test scores of the two groups:
\[
\boxed{\mu_1-\mu_2}.
\]
In words, this is the population mean test score for first-grade students using educational software minus the population mean test score for first-grade students not using it.
