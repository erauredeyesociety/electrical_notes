\section*{HW08 Question 15}

\textbf{Problem.} Guilty plea table:
\[
\begin{array}{c|cc}
 & \text{Guilty plea} & \text{Not guilty plea}\\ \hline
\text{Sent to prison} & 423 & 59\\
\text{Not sent to prison} & 549 & 13
\end{array}
\]

\textbf{Solution.}
\[
H_0:\text{plea and prison sentence are independent},\qquad
H_1:\text{they are not independent}.
\]
\[
\chi^2=\sum\frac{(O-E)^2}{E}=39.83,\qquad df=1.
\]
The P-value is approximately \(0.000\), so reject \(H_0\).

For context:
\[
P(\text{prison}\mid\text{guilty plea})=\frac{423}{423+549}=0.435,
\]
\[
P(\text{prison}\mid\text{not guilty plea})=\frac{59}{59+13}=0.819.
\]

\[
\boxed{\text{Choice C: sufficient evidence rejects independence, and the results encourage a guilty plea.}}
\]
