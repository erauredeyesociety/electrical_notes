\section*{HW08 Question 2}

\textbf{Problem.} Brand A and Brand B tire wear are compared using \(n_1=n_2=12\). Brand A has \(\bar{x}_1=38400,\ s_1=5200\). Brand B has \(\bar{x}_2=39700,\ s_2=5800\). Assume equal variances and test whether average wear differs.

\textbf{Solution.}
\[
H_0:\mu_1-\mu_2=0,\qquad H_1:\mu_1-\mu_2\ne0.
\]
\[
s_p=\sqrt{\frac{11(5200)^2+11(5800)^2}{22}}=5508.18.
\]
\[
t=\frac{38400-39700}{5508.18\sqrt{1/12+1/12}}=-0.58,\qquad df=22.
\]
The two-tailed P-value is \(0.569\).

\[
\boxed{\text{Do not reject }H_0. \text{ There is not enough evidence of different mean wear.}}
\]
