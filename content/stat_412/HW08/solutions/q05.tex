\section*{HW08 Question 5}

\textbf{Problem.} Organism densities at Cedar Run station 1 and station 2 are compared. Assume normal populations with different variances. Test at \(\alpha=0.05\) whether the average densities are equal.

\textbf{Known setup.}
\[
H_0:\mu_1-\mu_2=0,\qquad H_1:\mu_1-\mu_2\ne0.
\]

\textbf{Data summary.} For station 1,
\[
n_1=15,\qquad \bar{x}_1=9763.33,\qquad s_1=8131.75.
\]
For station 2,
\[
n_2=11,\qquad \bar{x}_2=4301.82,\qquad s_2=2516.02.
\]

\textbf{Test statistic.} Using Welch's two-sample test,
\[
t=\frac{\bar{x}_1-\bar{x}_2}{\sqrt{s_1^2/n_1+s_2^2/n_2}}
  =\frac{9763.33-4301.82}{\sqrt{8131.75^2/15+2516.02^2/11}}
  =2.446.
\]
The Welch degrees of freedom are \(df\approx17.48\).

\textbf{P-value and conclusion.} For the two-tailed test,
\[
p=2P(T_{17.48}\ge 2.446)\approx0.0253.
\]
Since \(p<0.05\), reject \(H_0\). The average densities at the two stations are not equal.

\[
\boxed{t=2.446,\quad df\approx17.48,\quad p\approx0.0253;\ \text{reject }H_0.}
\]
