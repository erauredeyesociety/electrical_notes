# Question 16

Contingency table for tennis challenges by gender:

| Gender | Successful challenge | Unsuccessful challenge |
|---|---:|---:|
| Men | 246 | 556 |
| Women | 475 | 680 |

## Solution

- \(H_0\): gender and challenge result are independent.
- \(H_1\): gender and challenge result are not independent.
- Test statistic: \(\chi^2=22.22\).
- P-value: approximately \(0.000\).
- Conclusion: reject \(H_0\). Gender and challenge result are not independent.



## Final multiple-choice conclusion

Use a 0.05 significance level to test the claim that the gender of the tennis player is independent of whether a call is overturned.

Answer: D. There is sufficient evidence to warrant rejection of the claim that the gender of the tennis player is independent of whether a call is overturned.
