# Question 18

Observed and expected distribution for the number of games played in a best-of-seven contest:

| Games played | Actual contests | Expected proportion |
|---:|---:|---:|
| 4 | 20 | 0.125 |
| 5 | 19 | 0.250 |
| 6 | 22 | 0.3125 |
| 7 | 36 | 0.3125 |

## Solution

- \(H_0\): the actual distribution fits the expected proportions.
- \(H_1\): the actual distribution does not fit the expected proportions.
- Total \(n=97\); expected counts are \(12.125,\ 24.250,\ 30.313,\ 30.313\).
- Test statistic: \(\chi^2=9.60\).
- P-value: \(0.0223\).
- Conclusion at \(\alpha=0.05\): reject \(H_0\). The actual distribution does not fit the expected proportions.

## Final multiple-choice conclusion

Answer: **B.** Reject \(H_0\). There is sufficient evidence to warrant rejection of the claim that the actual numbers of games fit the expected proportions.
