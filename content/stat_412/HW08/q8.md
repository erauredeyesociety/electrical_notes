# Question 8

A research group conducted a study of the effectiveness of educational software. In one phase of the study, a sample of 1,502 first-grade students in classrooms that used educational software was compared to a sample of 1,115 first-grade students in classrooms that did not use the technology. In its report, the group concluded that "mean test scores were significantly higher in classrooms using reading software products" than in classrooms that did not use educational software. Complete parts a through c below.

## Part (a)

Identify the parameter of interest to the research group. Let the first group be the 1,502 first-grade students who used the software, and let the second group be the 1,115 first-grade students who did not use the software.

## Solution

The parameter of interest is

\[
\mu_1-\mu_2,
\]

the difference between the population mean test score for first-grade students in classrooms using the software and the population mean test score for first-grade students in classrooms not using the software.
