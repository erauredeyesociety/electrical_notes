# Question 6

A random sample of 90 adults is classified according to gender and the number of hours of television watched during a week. Use a 0.01 level of significance and test the hypothesis that the time spent watching television is independent of whether the viewer is male or female.

## Hypothesis setup

- \(H_0\): the time spent watching television is independent of gender.
- \(H_1\): the time spent watching television is not independent of gender.

## Data

| Counts | Male | Female |
|---|---:|---:|
| Over 25 hours | 15 | 28 |
| Under 25 hours | 28 | 19 |

## Solution

The expected counts are:

\[
\begin{array}{c|cc}
 & \text{Male} & \text{Female}\\ \hline
\text{Over 25 hours} & 20.544 & 22.456\\
\text{Under 25 hours} & 22.456 & 24.544
\end{array}
\]

\[
\chi^2=5.487,\qquad df=1,\qquad p\approx0.0192.
\]

Since \(p>0.01\), fail to reject \(H_0\). There is not sufficient evidence at the 0.01 level to conclude that TV time and gender are dependent.
