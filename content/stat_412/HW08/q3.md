# Question 3

The following data represent the running times of films produced by two motion-picture companies. Test the hypothesis that the average running time of films produced by company 2 exceeds the average running time of films produced by company 1 by 10 minutes against the one-sided alternative that the difference is less than 10 minutes. Use a 0.05 level of significance and assume the distributions of times to be approximately normal with unequal variances.

Let sample 1 be the company 1 films and let sample 2 be the company 2 films.

| Company | Times (minutes) |
|---|---|
| 1 | 105, 86, 95, 99, 84 |
| 2 | 89, 156, 103, 141, 98, 79, 112 |

## Solution

- Platform hypotheses, written in the displayed form \( \mu_1-\mu_2 \): \(H_0:\mu_1-\mu_2=-10\), \(H_1:\mu_1-\mu_2>-10\).
- \(\bar{x}_1=93.80\), \(\bar{x}_2=111.14\), \(s_1=8.81\), \(s_2=27.89\).
- Welch test statistic in the platform's \(\mu_1-\mu_2\) orientation: \(t=-0.65\), \(df\approx7.57\).
- P-value: \(0.733\).
- Conclusion: do not reject \(H_0\). There is not sufficient evidence that the difference is less than 10 minutes.
