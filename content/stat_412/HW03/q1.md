# Question 1

Classify the following random variables as discrete or continuous:

- \(X\): the amount of rain in City B during April.
- \(Y\): the number of free-throw attempts before the first shot is made.
- \(M\): the weight of a T-bone steak.
- \(N\): the amount of snowfall.
- \(P\): the height of a randomly selected giraffe.

## Part 1

Is \(X\), "the amount of rain in City B during April," discrete or continuous?

- **The random variable is continuous.** ✓
- The random variable is discrete.

## Part 2

Is \(Y\), "the number of free-throw attempts before the first shot is made,"
discrete or continuous?

- **The random variable is discrete.** ✓
- The random variable is continuous.

## Part 3

Is \(M\), "the weight of a T-bone steak," discrete or continuous?

- The random variable is discrete.
- **The random variable is continuous.** ✓

## Part 4

Is \(N\), "the amount of snowfall," discrete or continuous?

- The random variable is discrete.
- **The random variable is continuous.** ✓

## Part 5

Is \(P\), "the height of a randomly selected giraffe," discrete or continuous?

- The random variable is discrete.
- **The random variable is continuous.** ✓

## Reasoning

A *count* of separate outcomes takes isolated integer values and is
**discrete**; a *measured amount* can take any value in an interval and is
**continuous**.

| Variable | Description | Type |
|----------|-------------|------|
| \(X\) | amount of rain (measured) | continuous |
| \(Y\) | number of attempts (count) | discrete |
| \(M\) | weight of steak (measured) | continuous |
| \(N\) | amount of snowfall (measured) | continuous |
| \(P\) | height of giraffe (measured) | continuous |
