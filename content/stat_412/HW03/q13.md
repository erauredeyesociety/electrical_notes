# Question 13

The loaves of rye bread distributed to local stores by a certain bakery have an
average length of \(31\) centimeters and a standard deviation of \(2\)
centimeters. Assuming that the lengths are normally distributed, complete
parts (a) through (c).

(a) What is the probability that a randomly selected loaf is longer than
\(33.2\) centimeters?

The probability is [answer]. *(Round to four decimal places as needed.)*

(b) What is the probability that a randomly selected loaf is between \(30.2\)
and \(34.5\) centimeters in length?

The probability is [answer]. *(Round to four decimal places as needed.)*

(c) What is the probability that a randomly selected loaf is shorter than
\(26.5\) centimeters?

The probability is [answer]. *(Round to four decimal places as needed.)*
