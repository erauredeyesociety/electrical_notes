\section*{Problem}
A bus arrives every 25 minutes at a bus stop. It is assumed that the waiting
time for a particular individual is a random variable with a continuous
uniform distribution.

(a) What is the probability that the individual waits more than 12 minutes?

(b) What is the probability that the individual waits between 4 and 12
minutes?

(a) The probability is \fbox{\strut\hspace{1.5em}}.

\textit{(Simplify your answer.)}

\section*{Work}
The waiting time is $X\sim\operatorname{Uniform}(0,25)$. Therefore
\[
P(X>12)=\frac{25-12}{25}=\frac{13}{25}=0.52,
\]
and
\[
P(4<X<12)=\frac{12-4}{25}=\frac8{25}=0.32.
\]

\finalanswer{(a) $0.52$ \qquad (b) $0.32$.}
