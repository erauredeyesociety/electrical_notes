\section*{Problem}
Based on extensive testing, it is determined by the manufacturer of a washing
machine that the time $Y$ (in years) before a major repair is required is
characterized by the probability density function.
\[
f(y)=
\begin{cases}
\dfrac18\exp\left(-\dfrac y8\right),&y\ge0,\\
0,&y<0.
\end{cases}
\]

(a) Critics would certainly consider the product a bargain if it is unlikely
to require a major repair before the tenth year. Consider a probability of less
than 0.35 to be unlikely. Comment on this by determining $P(Y>10)$.

(b) What is the probability that a major repair occurs in the first year?

than 0.35 to be unlikely. Comment on this by determining $P(Y>10)$.

Critics \fbox{\strut\hspace{2.5em}} consider the product a bargain because
$P(Y>10)={}$\fbox{\strut\hspace{1.5em}}.

\textit{(Round to four decimal places as needed.)}

\section*{Work}
Here $Y$ is exponential with rate $\lambda=1/8$. Its survival probability is
\[
P(Y>10)=e^{-10/8}=e^{-1.25}\approx0.2865.
\]
The platform asks for the conclusion by comparing this displayed probability
directly with $0.35$. Since $0.2865<0.35$, select \emph{would}. Also,
\[
P(Y\le1)=1-e^{-1/8}\approx0.1175.
\]

\finalanswer{(a) The critics \textbf{would} consider it a bargain because
$P(Y>10)=0.2865<0.35$. \quad (b) $0.1175$.}
