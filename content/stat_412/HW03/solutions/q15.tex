\section*{Problem}
A certain type of device has an advertised failure rate of 0.00625 per hour.
The failure rate is constant and the exponential distribution applies.

(a) What is the mean time to failure?

(b) What is the probability that 320 hours will pass before a failure is
observed?

(a) The mean time to failure is \fbox{\strut\hspace{1.5em}} hours.

\textit{(Type an integer or a decimal. Do not round.)}

\section*{Work}
For an exponential random variable with rate $\lambda=0.00625$,
\[
E(X)=\frac1\lambda=\frac1{0.00625}=160\text{ hours}.
\]
Its right-tail probability is
\[
P(X>320)=e^{-\lambda(320)}
=e^{-0.00625(320)}=e^{-2}\approx0.1353.
\]

\finalanswer{(a) $160$ hours \qquad (b) $0.1353$.}
