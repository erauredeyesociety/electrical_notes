\section*{Problem}
The shelf life, in days, for bottles of a certain prescribed medicine is a
random variable having the density function shown below. Find the probability
that a bottle of this medicine will have a shelf life of (a) at least 600 days;
(b) anywhere from 120 to 160 days.
\[
f(x)=
\begin{cases}
\dfrac{45{,}000}{(x+150)^3},&x>0,\\
0,&\text{elsewhere}.
\end{cases}
\]

(a) The probability of a shelf life of at least 600 days is
\fbox{\strut\hspace{1.5em}}.

\textit{(Type an integer or decimal rounded to three decimal places as needed.)}

\section*{Work}
For $a>0$,
\[
P(X\ge a)=\int_a^\infty\frac{45000}{(x+150)^3}\,dx
=\left[-\frac{22500}{(x+150)^2}\right]_a^\infty
=\frac{22500}{(a+150)^2}.
\]
Therefore
\[
P(X\ge600)=\frac{22500}{750^2}=0.040,
\]
and
\[
\begin{aligned}
P(120\le X\le160)
&=P(X\ge120)-P(X\ge160)\\
&=\frac{22500}{270^2}-\frac{22500}{310^2}\\
&\approx0.0746\approx0.075.
\end{aligned}
\]

\finalanswer{(a) $0.040$ \qquad (b) $0.075$.}
