\section*{Problem}
A certain type of device has an advertised failure rate of 0.0125 per hour. The
failure rate is constant and the exponential distribution applies with
$\beta=80$. What are the mean and variance of the time that elapses before 2
failures occur?

The mean is \fbox{\strut\hspace{1.5em}} hours.

\textit{(Type an integer or a decimal. Do not round.)}

\section*{Work}
The time $T$ until two failures is the sum of two independent exponential
waiting times. Thus $T$ is Erlang with shape $2$ and scale $\beta=80$:
\[
E(T)=2\beta=2(80)=160\text{ hours},
\]
\[
\operatorname{Var}(T)=2\beta^2=2(80)^2=12800\text{ hours}^2.
\]

\finalanswer{Mean $=160$ hours; variance $=12800$ hours$^2$.}
