\section*{Problem}
The proportion $Y$ of the budget for a certain type of industrial company that
is allotted to environmental and pollution control is coming under scrutiny. A
data collection project determines that the distribution of these proportions
is as shown. Complete parts (a) and (b).
\[
f(y)=
\begin{cases}
10(1-y)^9,&0\le y\le1,\\
0,&\text{elsewhere}.
\end{cases}
\]

(a) What is the mean proportion of the budget allocated to environmental and
pollution control?

\fbox{\strut\hspace{1.5em}} \textit{(Simplify your answer.)}

(b) What is the probability that a randomly selected company allocated a
proportion exceeding the population mean from part (a)? Round to four decimal
places.

\section*{Work}
\[
\begin{aligned}
E(Y)
&=\int_0^1y\,10(1-y)^9\,dy\\
&=10\operatorname{B}(2,10)
=10\frac{1!\,9!}{11!}
=\frac1{11}.
\end{aligned}
\]

For part (b),
\[
\begin{aligned}
P\left(Y>\frac1{11}\right)
&=\int_{1/11}^1 10(1-y)^9\,dy\\
&=\left(\frac{10}{11}\right)^{10}
\approx0.3855.
\end{aligned}
\]

\finalanswer{(a) $\displaystyle E(Y)=\frac1{11}$;
(b) $\displaystyle P\left(Y>\frac1{11}\right)=0.3855$.}
