\section*{Problem}
Classify the following random variables as discrete or continuous:
\begin{itemize}
\item X: the amount of rain in City B during April.
\item Y: the number of free-throw attempts before the first shot is made.
\item M: the weight of a T-bone steak.
\item N: the amount of snowfall.
\item $P$: the height of a randomly selected giraffe.
\end{itemize}

Is X: ``the amount of rain in City B during April'' discrete or continuous?
\begin{itemize}
\item The random variable is continuous.
\item The random variable is discrete.
\end{itemize}

\noindent
Is Y: ``the number of free-throw attempts before the first shot is made''
discrete or continuous?
\begin{itemize}
\item The random variable is discrete.
\item The random variable is continuous.
\end{itemize}

\noindent
Is M: ``the weight of a T-bone steak'' discrete or continuous?
\begin{itemize}
\item The random variable is discrete.
\item The random variable is continuous.
\end{itemize}

\noindent
Is N: ``the amount of snowfall'' discrete or continuous?
\begin{itemize}
\item The random variable is discrete.
\item The random variable is continuous.
\end{itemize}

\noindent
Is $P$: ``the height of a randomly selected giraffe'' discrete or continuous?
\begin{itemize}
\item The random variable is discrete.
\item The random variable is continuous.
\end{itemize}

\section*{Work}
A count has separated integer values and is discrete. A measured amount can
take any real value in an interval and is continuous.
\[
\begin{array}{cl}
\toprule
\text{Variable} & \text{Classification}\\
\midrule
X & \text{continuous}\\
Y & \text{discrete}\\
M & \text{continuous}\\
N & \text{continuous}\\
P & \text{continuous}\\
\bottomrule
\end{array}
\]

\finalanswer{$X$ is \textbf{continuous}; $Y$ is \textbf{discrete};
$M$ is \textbf{continuous}; $N$ is \textbf{continuous}; $P$ is
\textbf{continuous}.}
