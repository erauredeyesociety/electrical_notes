\section*{Problem}
The loaves of rye bread distributed to local stores by a certain bakery have an
average length of 31 centimeters and a standard deviation of 2 centimeters.
Assuming that the lengths are normally distributed, complete parts (a) through
(c) below.

Click here to view page 1 of the standard normal distribution table.

Click here to view page 2 of the standard normal distribution table.

(a) What is the probability that a randomly selected loaf is longer than 33.2
centimeters? \textit{(Round to four decimal places.)}

(b) What is the probability that a randomly selected loaf is between 30.2 and
34.5 centimeters in length? \textit{(Round to four decimal places.)}

(c) What is the probability that a randomly selected loaf is shorter than 26.5
centimeters? \textit{(Round to four decimal places.)}

\section*{Work}
\[
z=\frac{33.2-31}{2}=1.10,\qquad
P(X>33.2)=1-\Phi(1.10)=1-0.8643=0.1357.
\]

For part (b),
\[
z_{30.2}=\frac{30.2-31}{2}=-0.40,\qquad
z_{34.5}=\frac{34.5-31}{2}=1.75,
\]
\[
P(30.2<X<34.5)=\Phi(1.75)-\Phi(-0.40)
=0.9599-0.3446=0.6153.
\]

For part (c),
\[
z=\frac{26.5-31}{2}=-2.25,\qquad
P(X<26.5)=\Phi(-2.25)=0.0122.
\]

\finalanswer{(a) $0.1357$; (b) $0.6153$; (c) $0.0122$.}
