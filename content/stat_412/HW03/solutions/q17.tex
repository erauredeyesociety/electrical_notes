\section*{Problem}
The total number of hours, in units of 100 hours, that a family runs a vacuum
cleaner over a period of one year is a random variable $X$ having the density
function shown to the right. Find the variance of $X$.
\[
f(x)=
\begin{cases}
\left(\dfrac14\right)(x-1),&1<x<3,\\[3pt]
1-\dfrac14(x-1),&3\le x\le5,\\[3pt]
0,&\text{elsewhere}.
\end{cases}
\]

The variance is \fbox{\strut\hspace{1.5em}}.
\textit{(Round to three decimal places as needed.)}

\section*{Work}
The triangular density is symmetric about $x=3$, so $E(X)=3$. Since the
second branch equals $(5-x)/4$,
\[
\begin{aligned}
E(X^2)
&=\int_1^3x^2\frac{x-1}{4}\,dx
 +\int_3^5x^2\frac{5-x}{4}\,dx\\
&=\frac{29}{3}.
\end{aligned}
\]
Therefore
\[
\operatorname{Var}(X)=E(X^2)-[E(X)]^2
=\frac{29}{3}-9=\frac23\approx0.667.
\]

\finalanswer{$\displaystyle \operatorname{Var}(X)=\frac23\approx0.667$.}
