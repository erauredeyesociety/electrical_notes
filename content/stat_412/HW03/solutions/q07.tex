\section*{Problem}
For a laboratory assignment, if the equipment is working, the density function
of the observed outcome $X$ is as shown below. Find the variance and standard
deviation of $X$.
\[
f(x)=
\begin{cases}
\dfrac18(4-x),&0<x<4,\\
0,&\text{otherwise}.
\end{cases}
\]

The variance of $X$ is \fbox{\strut\hspace{1.5em}}. The standard deviation of
$X$ is \fbox{\strut\hspace{1.5em}}.

\textit{(Simplify your answers. Type exact answers, using radicals as needed.)}

\section*{Work}
\[
E(X)=\int_0^4x\frac{4-x}{8}\,dx=\frac43,
\qquad
E(X^2)=\int_0^4x^2\frac{4-x}{8}\,dx=\frac83.
\]
Therefore
\[
\operatorname{Var}(X)=\frac83-\left(\frac43\right)^2=\frac89,
\]
and
\[
\sigma_X=\sqrt{\operatorname{Var}(X)}
=\sqrt{\frac89}=\frac{2\sqrt2}{3}.
\]

\finalanswer{$\displaystyle \operatorname{Var}(X)=\frac89$ and
$\displaystyle \sigma_X=\frac{2\sqrt2}{3}$.}
