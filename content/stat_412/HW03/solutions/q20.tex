\section*{Problem}
If a set of observations is normally distributed, what percent of these differ
from the mean by

(a) more than $1.4\sigma$?

(b) less than $0.46\sigma$?

Click here to view page 1 of the standard normal distribution table.

Click here to view page 2 of the standard normal distribution table.

(a) \fbox{\strut\hspace{1.5em}}\%

\textit{(Round to two decimal places as needed.)}

\section*{Work}
After standardization and using symmetry,
\[
P(|Z|>1.4)=2P(Z>1.4)
=2[1-\Phi(1.4)]
=2(1-0.9192)=0.1616.
\]
Similarly,
\[
P(|Z|<0.46)=\Phi(0.46)-\Phi(-0.46)
=2\Phi(0.46)-1
=2(0.6772)-1=0.3544.
\]

\finalanswer{(a) $16.16\%$ \qquad (b) $35.44\%$.}
