\section*{Problem}
The time to failure in hours of an important piece of electronic equipment used
in a manufactured DVD player has the density below. Find the mean number of hours
to failure of the component.
\[
f(x)=
\begin{cases}
\dfrac{3}{1000}\,e^{-3x/1000},&x\ge 0,\\
0,&x<0.
\end{cases}
\]
\textit{(Type an integer or a simplified fraction.)}

\section*{Setup}
This matches the \textbf{exponential} form $f(x)=\lambda e^{-\lambda x}$ with
rate parameter
\[
\lambda=\frac{3}{1000}\text{ per hour.}
\]
For an exponential variable the rate is one over the mean ($\lambda=1/\mu$), so
the mean is simply the reciprocal of the rate.

\section*{Work}
\[
\mu=E(X)=\frac1\lambda=\frac{1}{3/1000}=\frac{1000}{3}\approx 333.33\text{ hours.}
\]
(Equivalently, $E(X)=\int_0^\infty x\,\tfrac{3}{1000}e^{-3x/1000}\,dx=\tfrac{1000}{3}$.)

\finalanswer{Mean time to failure $=\dfrac{1000}{3}\approx 333.33$ hours.}
