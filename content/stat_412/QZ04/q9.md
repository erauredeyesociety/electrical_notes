# Question 9

The joint density of \(X,Y,Z\) is

\[
f(x,y,z)=
\begin{cases}
\dfrac{3xy^2z}{25}, & 0<x<2,\ 0<y<1,\ 0<z<5,\\
0, & \text{elsewhere}.
\end{cases}
\]

Complete parts (a) through (d).

## (a)

Find the joint marginal density of \(Y\) and \(Z\). The choices are:

- **A.** \(g(x,z)=[\text{answer}]\), for \(0<x<2,\ 0<z<5\).
- **B.** \(g(x,y)=[\text{answer}]\), for \(0<x<2,\ 0<y<1\).
- **C.** \(g(y,z)=[\text{answer}]\), for \(0<y<1,\ 0<z<5\).

Select **Choice C**:

\[
\boxed{g(y,z)=\frac{6y^2z}{25}},
\qquad 0<y<1,\quad0<z<5.
\]

## (b)

Find the marginal density of \(Y\). The choices are:

- **A.** \(h(x)=[\text{answer}]\), for \(0<x<2\).
- **B.** \(h(z)=[\text{answer}]\), for \(0<z<5\).
- **C.** \(h(y)=[\text{answer}]\), for \(0<y<1\).

Select **Choice C**:

\[
\boxed{h(y)=3y^2},\qquad 0<y<1.
\]

## (c)

Find

\[
P\left(\frac14<X<\frac54,\ Y>\frac13,\ 3<Z<4\right).
\]

\[
\boxed{\frac{91}{900}}.
\]

## (d)

Find

\[
P\left(0<X<\frac34\mid Y=\frac12,\ Z=2\right).
\]

\[
\boxed{\frac9{64}}.
\]
