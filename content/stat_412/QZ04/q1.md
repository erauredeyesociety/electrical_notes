# Question 1

If \(X\) and \(Y\) have

\[
\sigma_X^2=3,\qquad \sigma_Y^2=9,\qquad \sigma_{XY}=4,
\]

and are not independent, find the variance of

\[
Z=-4X+6Y-8.
\]

(Simplify your answer.)

Using

\[
\operatorname{Var}(aX+bY+c)
=a^2\sigma_X^2+b^2\sigma_Y^2+2ab\sigma_{XY},
\]

\[
\sigma_Z^2
=16(3)+36(9)+2(-4)(6)(4)
=48+324-192
=\boxed{180}.
\]
