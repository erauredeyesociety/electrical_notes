# Question 5

The joint density of \(X\) and \(Y\) is

\[
f(x,y)=
\begin{cases}
\dfrac4{27}x^2, & 0<x<3,\ 0<y<3-x,\\
0, & \text{elsewhere}.
\end{cases}
\]

(a) Show that \(X\) and \(Y\) are not independent.

(b) Find \(P(X>0.3\mid Y=1.7)\). Round to six decimal places as needed.

## (a)

Select the correct statement and fill in its conditional-density box:

- **A.** \(f(x\mid y)=f(x,y)/h(y)\), for \(0<x<3-y\), involves \(y\).
- **B.** \(f(x\mid y)=f(x,y)/h(y)\), for \(0<x<3-y\), is a function only of
  \(x\).
- **C.** \(f(x\mid y)=f(x,y)/h(y)\), for \(0<y<3-x\), involves \(x\).
- **D.** \(f(x\mid y)=f(x,y)/h(y)\), for \(0<x<3-y\), is constant.

Select **Choice A** and enter

\[
\boxed{f(x\mid y)=\frac{3x^2}{(3-y)^3}},
\qquad 0<x<3-y.
\]

The conditional density involves \(y\), so \(X\) and \(Y\) are not independent.

## (b)

\[
\boxed{P(X>0.3\mid Y=1.7)=\frac{2170}{2197}\approx0.987711}.
\]
