# Question 4

A carpet-cleaning machine delivers \(Y\) gallons of cleaner per minute, with

\[
f(y)=
\begin{cases}
\frac14, & 7\le y\le11,\\
0, & \text{elsewhere}.
\end{cases}
\]

Complete parts (a) and (b).

## (a)

Choose the graph of the density:

- **A.** A horizontal segment from \((7,0.25)\) to \((11,0.25)\).
- **B.** A vertical segment from \((0.25,7)\) to \((0.25,11)\).
- **C.** A line segment from \((7,0)\) to \((11,1)\).
- **D.** A horizontal segment from \((0,0.25)\) to \((12,0.25)\).

\[
\boxed{\text{Choice A}}.
\]

## (b)

Find \(E(Y)\), \(E(Y^2)\), and \(\operatorname{Var}(Y)\).

\[
\boxed{E(Y)=9},\qquad
\boxed{E(Y^2)=\frac{247}{3}},\qquad
\boxed{\operatorname{Var}(Y)=\frac43}.
\]
