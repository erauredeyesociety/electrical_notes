# Question 2

A tobacco company produces blends containing Turkish, domestic, and other
tobaccos. Let \(X\) and \(Y\) be the proportions of Turkish and domestic
tobacco. Their joint density is

\[
f(x,y)=
\begin{cases}
24xy, & 0\le x\le1,\ 0\le y\le1,\ x+y\le1,\\
0, & \text{elsewhere}.
\end{cases}
\]

Complete parts (a) through (c).

## (a)

Find \(P(X\ge4/9)\).

\[
\boxed{P(X\ge4/9)=\frac{875}{2187}}.
\]

## (b)

Find the marginal density of the domestic proportion.

Enter \(h(y)\), then select its support from:

- \(0\le y\le1-x\)
- \(x\le y\le1\)
- \(x\le y\le1-x\)
- \(0\le y\le1\)

\[
\boxed{h(y)=12y(1-y)^2,\qquad 0\le y\le1}
\]

and \(h(y)=0\) elsewhere. Select support \(0\le y\le1\).

## (c)

Find

\[
P\left(X<\frac17\mid Y=\frac34\right).
\]

\[
\boxed{\frac{16}{49}}.
\]
