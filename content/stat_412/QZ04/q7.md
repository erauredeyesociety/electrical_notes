# Question 7

A fast-food restaurant has drive-through and walk-in facilities. Let \(X\) and
\(Y\) be the proportions of time that the drive-through and walk-in facilities
are in use. Their joint density is

\[
f(x,y)=
\begin{cases}
\dfrac29(5x+4y), & 0\le x\le1,\ 0\le y\le1,\\
0, & \text{elsewhere}.
\end{cases}
\]

Complete parts (a) through (c).

## (a)

Find the marginal density of \(X\). The choices are:

- **A.** \(g(x)=[\text{answer}]\), for \(0\le x\le1\).
- **B.** \(h(y)=[\text{answer}]\), for \(0\le y\le1\).

Select **Choice A**:

\[
\boxed{g(x)=\frac{10x+4}{9},\qquad 0\le x\le1}.
\]

## (b)

Find the marginal density of \(Y\). The choices are:

- **A.** \(g(x)=[\text{answer}]\), for \(0\le x\le1\).
- **B.** \(h(y)=[\text{answer}]\), for \(0\le y\le1\).

Select **Choice B**:

\[
\boxed{h(y)=\frac{8y+5}{9},\qquad 0\le y\le1}.
\]

## (c)

Find the probability that the drive-through is busy less than one-third of the
time.

\[
\boxed{P(X<1/3)=\frac{17}{81}}.
\]
