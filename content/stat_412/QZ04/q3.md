# Question 3

For a laboratory assignment, if the equipment is working, the observed outcome
\(X\) has density

\[
f(x)=
\begin{cases}
8\left(\frac12-x\right), & 0<x<\frac12,\\
0, & \text{otherwise}.
\end{cases}
\]

Find the variance and standard deviation of \(X\). Simplify and use exact
radicals as needed.

\[
\boxed{\operatorname{Var}(X)=\frac1{72}},\qquad
\boxed{\sigma_X=\frac{\sqrt2}{12}}.
\]
