# Question 6

The annual number \(X\) of power failures has the distribution

| \(x\) | 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| \(P(X=x)\) | 0.4 | 0.1 | 0.2 | 0.3 |

The mean and variance are

\[
E(X)=1.4,\qquad \sigma_X^2=1.64.
\]

Find the mean and variance of

\[
Z=7X+9.
\]

Do not round.

\[
\boxed{E(Z)=18.8},\qquad
\boxed{\sigma_Z^2=80.36}.
\]
