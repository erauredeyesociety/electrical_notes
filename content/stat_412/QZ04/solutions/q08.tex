\section*{Problem}
A light bulb has mean life $700$ hours and standard deviation $50$ hours.
At most what percentage fail to last $600$ hours? Assume the distribution is
symmetric about the mean.

\section*{Work}
The cutoff is
\[
\frac{700-600}{50}=2
\]
standard deviations below the mean. Chebyshev gives
\[
P(|X-700|\ge100)\le\frac1{2^2}=\frac14.
\]
Because the distribution is symmetric, the lower tail is at most half of the
two-tail bound:
\[
P(X\le600)\le\frac12\left(\frac14\right)=\frac18=12.5\%.
\]

\finalanswer{At most $12.5\%$; enter $12.5$.}
