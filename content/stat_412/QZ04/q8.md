# Question 8

A 100-watt light bulb has mean life \(700\) hours and standard deviation
\(50\) hours. At most what percentage of bulbs fail to last \(600\) hours?
Assume the distribution is symmetric about the mean.

Since \(600\) is two standard deviations below the mean, Chebyshev gives a
two-tail bound of \(1/2^2=1/4\). Symmetry assigns at most half to the lower
tail:

\[
\boxed{12.5\%}.
\]

Type \(12.5\); do not round.
