\section*{HW09 Question 3}

\textbf{Problem.} Vinyl gloves are population 1. Among \(241\) vinyl gloves, \(61\%\) leaked viruses. Among \(241\) latex gloves, \(11\%\) leaked viruses. Use the technology output at \(\alpha=0.10\) to test whether vinyl gloves have a greater leak rate.

\[
H_0:p_1=p_2,\qquad H_a:p_1>p_2.
\]

Technology output gives pooled proportion \(0.36\), \(z=11.2997\), critical \(z=1.2816\), and \(p=0.0000\). The 80\% confidence interval is
\[
0.4458 < p_1-p_2 < 0.5418.
\]
Since \(p<0.10\), reject \(H_0\). There is sufficient evidence to support the claim that vinyl gloves have a greater virus leak rate than latex gloves.

\[
\boxed{\text{Choice B; reject }H_0;\ \text{vinyl gloves have a greater leak rate.}}
\]
