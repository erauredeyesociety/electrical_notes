\section*{HW09 Question 6}

\textbf{Problem.} Not wearing seat belts: \(31/2876\) killed. Wearing seat belts: \(17/7647\) killed. Test whether seat belts reduce fatalities at \(\alpha=0.01\).

\[
H_0:p_1=p_2,\qquad H_a:p_1>p_2.
\]
\[
\hat p_1=0.01078,\quad \hat p_2=0.00222,\quad z=5.80,\quad p\approx0.0000.
\]
Since the P-value is less than \(\alpha=0.01\), reject \(H_0\). There is sufficient evidence to support the claim that the fatality rate is higher for those not wearing seat belts.

For the right-tailed test at \(\alpha=0.01\), an appropriate 98\% CI is
\[
0.0039 < p_1-p_2 < 0.0132.
\]
Since the interval is entirely above 0, the confidence-interval method also supports the claim.
Platform CI dropdowns: do not include; not equal; only positive; higher.

\[
\boxed{\text{Choice D; reject }H_0;\ \text{seat belts appear effective in reducing fatalities.}}
\]
