\section*{HW09 Question 7}

\textbf{Problem.} Echinacea group: \(35/40\) infected. Placebo group: \(96/113\) infected. Test whether echinacea has an effect at \(\alpha=0.01\).

\[
H_0:p_1=p_2,\qquad H_a:p_1\ne p_2.
\]
\[
\hat p_1=0.8750,\quad \hat p_2=0.8496,\quad z=0.39,\quad p=0.6935.
\]
Since \(p=0.6935>0.01\), fail to reject \(H_0\). There is not sufficient evidence to support the claim that echinacea treatment has an effect.

For the two-tailed test at \(\alpha=0.01\), an appropriate 99\% CI is
\[
-0.1347 < p_1-p_2 < 0.1856.
\]
Because the interval contains 0, the confidence-interval method agrees with the test conclusion.
Platform CI dropdowns: include; does not; is not.

\[
\boxed{\text{Choice A; fail to reject }H_0.}
\]
