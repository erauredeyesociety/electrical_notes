\section*{HW09 Question 8}

\textbf{Problem.} Bednets: \(15/334\) developed malaria. No bednets: \(27/276\). Test whether malaria incidence is lower with bednets at \(\alpha=0.05\).

\[
\boxed{\text{Choice E: }H_0:p_1=p_2,\qquad H_a:p_1<p_2.}
\]
\[
\hat p_1=0.0449,\quad \hat p_2=0.0978,\quad z=-2.57,\quad p=0.0051.
\]
Since \(p=0.0051<0.05\), reject \(H_0\). There is sufficient evidence that infants using bednets have a lower malaria incidence rate. An appropriate 90\% confidence interval is
\[
-0.0877<p_1-p_2<-0.0181.
\]
Because the interval is entirely below 0, it also supports \(p_1<p_2\).

Platform CI entries rounded to four decimals:
\[
-0.0877 < p_1-p_2 < -0.0181.
\]
Final dropdowns for part (c): sufficient; appear; sufficient; appear. The hypothesis test and the confidence interval agree because both support \(p_1<p_2\).

\[
\boxed{\text{Choice E; reject }H_0;\ \text{bednets appear effective.}}
\]
