\section*{HW09 Question 10}

\textbf{Problem.} Male students: \(312/904\) had blue eyes. Female students: \(407/1106\). Test the claim that the proportions are the same at \(\alpha=0.01\).

\[
\boxed{\text{Choice C: }H_0:p_1=p_2,\qquad H_a:p_1\ne p_2.}
\]
\[
\hat p_1=0.3451,\quad \hat p_2=0.3680,\quad z=-1.06,\quad p=0.2875.
\]
Since \(p=0.2875>0.01\), fail to reject \(H_0\). There is not sufficient evidence to warrant rejection of the claim that the proportions of blue eyes are the same for females and males. The 99\% confidence interval is
\[
-0.0781<p_1-p_2<0.0324,
\]
which contains 0.

Because the interval contains 0, there is not a significant difference between the two proportions, and there is not sufficient evidence to warrant rejection of the equal-proportions claim.
This agrees with failing to reject \(H_0\): a zero difference remains plausible.

\[
\boxed{\text{Choice C; fail to reject }H_0;\ \text{same proportions remain plausible.}}
\]
