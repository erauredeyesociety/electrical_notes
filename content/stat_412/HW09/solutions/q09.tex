\section*{HW09 Question 9}

\textbf{Problem.} Passenger cars: \(245/2120\) had only rear plates. Commercial trucks: \(56/375\). Test whether commercial trucks have a higher violation rate at \(\alpha=0.01\).

\[
H_0:p_1=p_2,\qquad H_a:p_1<p_2.
\]
\[
\hat p_1=0.1156,\quad \hat p_2=0.1493,\quad z=-1.85,\quad p=0.0321.
\]
Since \(0.0321>0.01\), fail to reject \(H_0\). There is not sufficient evidence to support the claim that commercial truck owners violate laws requiring front license plates at a higher rate than owners of passenger cars. The 98\% confidence interval is
\[
-0.0795<p_1-p_2<0.0120,
\]
which contains 0.

Confidence-interval conclusion dropdowns: contains; is not; is not. Since 0 is plausible, the interval does not provide sufficient support that commercial truck owners have the higher violation rate.

\[
\boxed{\text{Choice A; fail to reject }H_0;\ \text{claim not supported at }0.01.}
\]
