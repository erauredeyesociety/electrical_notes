\section*{HW09 Question 2}

\textbf{Problem.} Less than two years married: \(251/300\) planned children. Five years or longer: \(308/400\). Test whether the first proportion is higher.

\[
H_0:p_1=p_2,\qquad H_a:p_1>p_2.
\]
\[
\hat p_1=0.8367,\quad \hat p_2=0.7700,\quad \hat p=\frac{559}{700}=0.7986.
\]
\[
z=\frac{0.8367-0.7700}{\sqrt{0.7986(0.2014)(1/300+1/400)}}=2.18.
\]
Right-tailed \(p=0.0148<0.05\), so reject \(H_0\). There is sufficient evidence to reject equality of the two proportions, so it can be concluded that the proportion is higher for wives married less than two years.

\[
\boxed{\text{Choice A; }z=2.18,\ p=0.0148;\ \text{sufficient evidence }p_1>p_2.}
\]
