\section*{HW09 Question 4}

\textbf{Problem.} Men: \(421/1405\) successful challenges. Women: \(227/775\). Test the claim that success rates are equal at \(\alpha=0.05\).

\[
H_0:p_1=p_2,\qquad H_a:p_1\ne p_2.
\]
\[
\hat p_1=0.2996,\quad \hat p_2=0.2929,\quad z=0.33,\quad p=0.7417.
\]
Since \(p=0.7417>0.05\), fail to reject \(H_0\). There is not sufficient evidence to warrant rejection of the claim that women and men have equal success in challenging calls. The conclusion dropdowns are: greater than; fail to reject; is not sufficient. The appropriate 95\% CI is
\[
-0.0333<p_1-p_2<0.0467,
\]
which contains 0.

Because the interval contains 0, there is not a significant difference between the two proportions.
Platform CI dropdowns: include; does not; is not sufficient.

\[
\boxed{\text{Choice C; fail to reject }H_0;\ \text{equal success rates remain plausible.}}
\]
