\section*{HW09 Question 1}

\textbf{Problem.} Urban residents: \(53\) of \(80\) favor the nuclear plant. Suburban residents: \(59\) of \(100\) favor it. Test whether the proportions differ.

\[
H_0:p_1=p_2,\qquad H_a:p_1\ne p_2.
\]

\[
\hat p_1=\frac{53}{80}=0.6625,\quad \hat p_2=\frac{59}{100}=0.5900,\quad
\hat p=\frac{112}{180}=0.6222.
\]
\[
z=\frac{\hat p_1-\hat p_2}{\sqrt{\hat p(1-\hat p)(1/n_1+1/n_2)}}=
\frac{0.6625-0.5900}{\sqrt{0.6222(0.3778)(1/80+1/100)}}=1.00.
\]
Two-tailed \(p=0.3188\). Since the P-value is greater than ordinary significance levels such as \(0.05\), fail to reject \(H_0\). There is not a significant difference between the urban and suburban proportions. In general, \(H_0\) would be rejected only for significance levels greater than the P-value.

\[
\boxed{\text{Choice E; }z=1.00,\ p=0.3188;\ \text{not enough evidence of a difference.}}
\]
