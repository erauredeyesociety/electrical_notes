# Question 3

A newspaper published an article about a study in which researchers subjected laboratory gloves to stress. Among 241 vinyl gloves, 61% leaked viruses. Among 241 latex gloves, 11% leaked viruses. Using the accompanying technology results, and using a 0.10 significance level, test the claim that vinyl gloves have a greater virus leak rate than latex gloves. Let vinyl gloves be population 1.

## Hypotheses

- \(H_0:p_1=p_2\)
- \(H_a:p_1>p_2\)

## Technology results

- Pooled proportion: \(0.36\)
- Test statistic: \(z=11.2997\)
- Critical value: \(z=1.2816\)
- P-value: \(0.0000\)
- 80% confidence interval:

\[
0.4458 < p_1-p_2 < 0.5418
\]

## Solution

Because the P-value is \(0.0000<0.10\), reject \(H_0\). There is sufficient evidence to support the claim that vinyl gloves have a greater virus leak rate than latex gloves.

- Completed sentence: The P-value is **less than** the significance level \(\alpha=0.10\), so **reject** the null hypothesis. There is **sufficient** evidence to support the claim that vinyl gloves have a greater virus leak rate than latex gloves.
