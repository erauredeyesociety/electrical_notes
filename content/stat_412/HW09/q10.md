# Question 10

Professors collected data consisting of eye color and gender of statistics students. Among 904 male students, 312 had blue eyes. Among 1106 female students, 407 had blue eyes. Use a 0.01 significance level to test the claim that the proportions of blue eyes are the same for males and females. Complete parts (a) through (c) below. Consider the first sample to be the sample of males and the second sample to be the sample of females.

## Part (a)

Hypothesis choice: **C**.

- \(H_0:p_1=p_2\)
- \(H_a:p_1\ne p_2\)

## Solution

\[
\hat p_1=\frac{312}{904}=0.3451,\qquad
\hat p_2=\frac{407}{1106}=0.3680.
\]

\[
z=-1.06,\qquad p=0.2875.
\]

Fail to reject \(H_0\). There is not sufficient evidence to reject the claim that the proportions of blue eyes are the same for males and females.

An appropriate 99% confidence interval for \(p_1-p_2\) is

\[
-0.0781 < p_1-p_2 < 0.0324.
\]

Because the interval contains 0, it agrees with the hypothesis-test conclusion.

## Platform confidence-interval conclusion

- The confidence interval **contains** 0.
- There **is not** a significant difference between the two proportions.
- There **is not sufficient** evidence to warrant rejection of the claim that the proportions of blue eyes are the same for females and males.
- Detailed reason: Since 0 is inside the interval, a difference of 0 remains plausible. That agrees with failing to reject \(H_0\); the data do not provide enough evidence that the male and female blue-eye proportions differ.

## Platform conclusion

- Dropdown options: **less than** / **greater than**. Select **greater than**.
- Dropdown options: **reject** / **fail to reject**. Select **fail to reject**.
- Dropdown options: **is sufficient** / **is not sufficient**. Select **is not sufficient**.
- Completed sentence: The P-value is **greater than** the significance level of \(\alpha=0.01\), so **fail to reject** the null hypothesis. There **is not sufficient** evidence to warrant rejection of the claim that the proportions of blue eyes are the same for females and males.
