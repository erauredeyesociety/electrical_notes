# Question 4

Since an instant replay system for tennis was introduced at a major tournament, men challenged 1405 referee calls, with the result that 421 of the calls were overturned. Women challenged 775 referee calls, and 227 of the calls were overturned. Use a 0.05 significance level to test the claim that men and women have equal success in challenging calls.

Let men be population 1 and women be population 2.

## Hypotheses

- \(H_0:p_1=p_2\)
- \(H_a:p_1\ne p_2\)

## Solution

\[
\hat p_1=\frac{421}{1405}=0.2996,\qquad
\hat p_2=\frac{227}{775}=0.2929.
\]

\[
z=0.33,\qquad p=0.7417.
\]

Fail to reject \(H_0\). There is not sufficient evidence to reject the claim that men and women have equal success rates in challenging calls.

## Platform answer

- Hypothesis choice: **C**.
- Dropdown options: **greater than** / **less than**. Select **greater than** because \(0.7417>0.05\).
- Dropdown options: **reject** / **fail to reject**. Select **fail to reject**.
- Dropdown options: **is sufficient** / **is not sufficient**. Select **is not sufficient**.
- Since \(p=0.7417>0.05\), **fail to reject** \(H_0\).
- Conclusion wording: There **is not sufficient** evidence to warrant rejection of the claim that women and men have equal success in challenging calls.

## Confidence interval check

An appropriate 95% confidence interval for \(p_1-p_2\) is

\[
-0.0333 < p_1-p_2 < 0.0467.
\]

Because the interval **contains** 0, there **is not** a significant difference between the two proportions, and there is not sufficient evidence to reject the equal-success claim.
