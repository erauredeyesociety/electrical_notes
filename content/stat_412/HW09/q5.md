A study was conducted to determine the proportion of people who dream in black and white instead of color. Among 286 people over the age of 55, 63 dream in black and white, and among 312 people under the age of 25, 132 dream in black and white. Use a 0.05 significance level to test the claim that the proportion of people over 55 who dream in black and white is greater than the proportion for those under 25.

Let people over age 55 be population 1 and people under age 25 be population 2.

## Hypotheses

- Hypothesis choice: **D**
- \(H_0:p_1\le p_2\)

\[

\[
z=\frac{0.2203-0.4231}{\sqrt{0.3261(1-0.3261)(1/286+1/312)}}=-5.284.
\]
