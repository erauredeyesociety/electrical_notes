# Plans — INDEX

Tactical *how* artifacts. [../roadmap.md](../roadmap.md) points here by path; detail never goes in the
roadmap.

**Naming by lifetime.** A point-in-time plan (a migration, a trade study) is dated
`YYYY-MM-DD-<slug>.md`. A continuously-updated living document is named by concept only — a date in the
filename of a document that never stops changing invites a near-duplicate later.

Delivered plans move to [../archives/plans/](../archives/plans/INDEX.md).

**EMPTY, and deliberately.** The work the roadmap currently names is small and sequential: point two
`prompt.md` files at a directive, resolve two preambles, file four loose files. None of it needs a
paragraph of design, so none of it gets a plan file —
[../directives/roadmap-and-plans.md](../directives/roadmap-and-plans.md).

**Write a plan here when** something needs a paragraph the roadmap must not carry. The first likely
candidate is the preamble migration ([../DRIFT_REPORT.md](../DRIFT_REPORT.md) § 2): six tracked `.tex`
files `\input` a preamble that is scheduled to be superseded, and retiring it without re-pointing and
rebuilding them first breaks a doctrine assignment.
