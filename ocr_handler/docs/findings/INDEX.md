| [corpus-census-2026-09-04.md](./corpus-census-2026-09-04.md) | **430 PDFs / 7,146 pages measured.** 67% of pages and 50% of documents need no OCR at all; 482 pages must never be sent to a model *(**corrected to 24** — see the row below)*; 51 documents carry a *corrupt* text layer (letter-spacing); only 2 documents in the whole repo are ink-annotated. The measurement that re-sequenced the roadmap by leverage. |
| [layout-mode-decides-whether-math-survives.md](./layout-mode-decides-whether-math-survives.md) | **Whether maths survives a born-digital PDF is decided by the extraction MODE, not by OCR** — `pdftotext -layout` keeps the 2-D geometry, plain extraction shreds it, and neither raises an error. Exposed and then closed a silent false negative in `inspect`: 47/47 pages scored `ok` while every equation was rubble. Now two axes, density and structural faithfulness ([plan](../plans/structural-faithfulness.md)). Also found the letter-spacing detector to be a ~17× overcount — 46 of its 51 documents were plot tick labels, because `\s` matches a newline. |
| [ground-truth-sample.md](./ground-truth-sample.md) | **161 pages hand-labelled, stratified** — the sample four decisions were waiting on. `shredded-lines` precision **84% strict / 98% broad** [71,92], but **recall ≈36% and that is an upper bound**: it is a high-precision *sampler*, not a census (a flattened fraction makes 2–3 short lines; `_SHRED_MIN_RUN=4` needs four). The 400-char gate should **not** be raised — 0 of 22 pages in the 400–800 band need OCR — but `image_area ≥ 20% OR drawings ≥ 40` cuts routed pages 2,353 → 1,689 (−28%) while lifting precision 35% → 52–60%. **36 pages carry maths the text layer turned into a false statement, and every one scores `ok`.** |
| [one-classifier-2026-09-06.md](./one-classifier-2026-09-06.md) | **Converging `pdfops` onto `textlayer` changes 428 of 7,187 pages, and the two causes are different in kind.** 35 come from the library swap and are threshold noise — 24 of those sit within 20 characters of the 400 gate, and the two libraries disagree only about whitespace policy (poppler collapses it, PyMuPDF keeps it). The other 406 are a **correction**: testing images alone called 405 pages `blank` that are full of *vector* content, one of them 0 characters, 0 images, **1,837 paths** and 8.9% non-white pixels. **The `482 blank pages` figure was a conflation; the real skip set is 24.** `get_images()` also misses an image painted from inside a Form XObject (14 pages, 1 of them routed `blank`). No page moves toward `blank`. All four must-not-break invariants checked by measurement: output bytes and every document/page verdict identical across 448 documents. |
| [recall-was-a-symbol-test-not-a-run-length.md](./recall-was-a-symbol-test-not-a-run-length.md) | **`shredded-lines` recall 35% → 68% while precision ROSE 85% → 93%** — the run length was never the binding constraint. `_SHRED_MIN_RUN` 4 → 2 alone costs 26 points of precision (the increment it adds hand-labels **28% genuine** — Wingdings bullet columns that map to ASCII `z`, and assembly gutters); requiring every run to carry one genuine *mathematical* character pays for all of it and removes the sample's only hard false positive. 75 new hand labels. Also: 7 of the 12 known recall misses are blocked by `_SHRED_MAX_CHARS`, not the run length — including the one the ground-truth sample named as the sharpest case; the plan's `cec_320`/`stat_412` "control corpus" is full of real flattened mathematics (44 of 49 hits are genuine) and was never a control; and the `ti` ligature is not dropped at all — it arrives as U+0017 and `unmapped-glyphs` already catches it on 89 of 92 pages. |
| [mask-separability-by-stratum-2026-09-06.md](./mask-separability-by-stratum-2026-09-06.md) | **The mask trick survives P2 and P3 — the constants do not.** `dark_mask` separates P2's handwriting from its graph-paper grid by **62 grey levels, 0 hits on 2,739,777 grid pixels**, and P3's blue ballpoint separates from black print with **0 false positives on 256,100 labelled printed pixels at every margin from 10 to 55**. But `RED_MARGIN = 55` transposed to blue **destroys** P3's handwriting — the composite comes out a broken dotted skeleton, invisibly at page scale and obviously at crop scale — and a second P3 document (`ps160/m17/M17 Review.pdf`, whose *printed* text is navy) leaks whole paragraphs at the relaxed margin — resolved by the finding that the **locator and the paint are two jobs with opposite tolerances** and want different constants (`locator=blue(55)`, `paint=blue(25)\|dark`). Three further corrections: **P1 is one document, not two** (`dsp-lctr1-…-plw.pdf` has no handwriting; its red pixels are an arrow); **`diff_mask` needs no twin** — PDF Annotator files carry an **optional content group**, and rendering it off reproduces the real twin diff at **IoU 1.0000**; and `merge_px = 64`'s plateau is a property of P1 alone — on P2 it merges half a page into one crop. Closes the recorded `/Ink`-vs-raster contradiction: **0 `/Subtype/Ink` annotations, 12 extra raster XObjects**; the ink is pixels. |
| [precision-replicates-the-priced-cost-does-not-2026-09-06.md](./precision-replicates-the-priced-cost-does-not-2026-09-06.md) | **The 93% precision claim replicates on documents nobody had labelled — the *price* was understated by 43% and misdescribed.** 115 new hand labels. Strict precision on 50 flagged pages from 35 unseen documents is **90.0% [79, 96]**, and **88.9% [77, 95]** on 45 pages whose *content* is absent from both label sets (21 of the 83 "unseen" documents turn out to be exact duplicates of labelled ones); clustered on documents the floor is ~80%. But the change orphans **66 corpus pages, not ~46**, and a read sample says **~35% of them [18, 57] are flattened mathematics** — Σ and ∫ with limits, `√(3kT/m)`, the lens equation, a 3×3 matrix product. `_is_strong` is blind in the direction of its own target: `∫`→`Z`, `√`→`r`, `Σ`→`X` leave no mathematical character behind. Also: the `-plw` population enumerated (22 pages / 2 docs, **7** flagged not 5) and **neither file contains a single `/Ink` annotation** — the corpus's only one is an unlooked-at student homework; a **67-page control corpus for `_SHRED_MAX_CHARS`, every page read**, which also shows a maths-free control is *vacuous* against `_is_strong`; and `_is_strong` accepting the very PUA glyphs `unmapped-glyphs` calls benign (29 pages). |
